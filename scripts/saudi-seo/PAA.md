# Questions Saudi parents actually ask

A **starting pool**, organised by the twelve `faqLanes` themes plan.ts assigns three of per page. Your brief names
your lane; draw from it, then make each question specific to your page — your subject, your
level, your district, your school cluster.

**The gate fails any question already used on another page in this set.** With 2,492 pages and
14+ FAQs each, a generic question is gone within the first few pages of a wave. Treat every
entry below as a *shape* to specialise, not a question to copy:

- Generic (will collide): "How much does an IB tutor cost in Saudi Arabia?"
- Specialised (yours): "Why does a Mathematics AA HL tutor cost more than a Mathematics AI SL one
  in Riyadh?"

Specialise by adding: the subject, the paper, the level, the district, the school type, the year
group, the series, the delivery format, or the specific situation (mid-course arrival, resit,
predicted grade dispute, curriculum switch).

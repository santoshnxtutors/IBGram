## What is the same for IGCSE tutoring everywhere in the NCR?

The syllabus, the exam series and the standard for a good tutor are the same whether you live in Dwarka or Dadri. That is worth fixing in mind first, because many families assume that a tutor's location changes the teaching. It does not. It changes the logistics.

**The qualification.** IGCSE in India means Cambridge IGCSE (Cambridge International Education) or Pearson Edexcel International GCSE, usually a two-year course across Grades 9 and 10. It is often followed by the International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Programme (DP), Cambridge International AS and A Levels, or a move to CBSE or ISC in Class 11.

**The exam series.** Cambridge series run in February/March (offered only in India), May/June and October/November. Pearson Edexcel series run in January and May/June. The school decides which series a student sits; a family cannot choose independently.

**The grades and tiers.** Cambridge grades most syllabuses A* to G, and some have 9-1 versions under different codes; Edexcel International GCSE grades 9-1. Many Cambridge subjects have Core and Extended tiers, with Core topping out at grade C and Extended reaching A*.

:::stats IGCSE in India at a glance
2 | Boards: Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel
3 | Cambridge series: Feb/Mar, May/Jun, Oct/Nov
A*-G | Cambridge grades; Edexcel uses 9-1
:::

**What a good tutor does.** A good IGCSE tutor knows the syllabus code the student is on, diagnoses before teaching, practises past papers with the markscheme, builds an error log and reviews progress at a set point. None of that depends on the city. The questions to ask a candidate are in [how to choose the right IGCSE tutor in Gurgaon](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-igcse-tutor-in-gurgaon/), and they work equally well in Noida or South Delhi.

So the real decision is about how the tutoring reaches your child.

## How do you choose between home, online and hybrid IGCSE tutoring?

Choose home tutoring if the tutor can reach you without a long evening journey and your child concentrates better with a person in the room. Choose online if the right specialist is not nearby or your child's week has no slack. Choose hybrid if one subject needs presence and the others do not. The choice is usually decided by three practical questions, not by principle.

:::steps Deciding the mode
Supply | Is a specialist within reach?
Journey | Can the tutor arrive on time?
Attention | Does your child focus on screens?
Subject | Does it need pen-and-paper presence?
:::

| Question | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|
| Is a tutor for the exact board, tier and subject within reasonable reach? | Home is realistic | Online widens the pool |
| Are weekday evening journeys reliable? | Home works | Online or a weekend slot |
| Does your child lose focus on video after 40 minutes? | Home, or shorter online blocks | Online is fine |
| Does the subject depend on diagrams and working that are easy to share? | Online works with a shared board | Home may be easier |
| Is a quiet room available? | Either works | Plan around when the room is free |

Two honest observations follow from the table. First, the answer can differ by subject: a student might have a home tutor for Mathematics, which benefits from presence, and an online tutor for Economics, which does not. Second, the answer changes through the year. In the final weeks before an exam series, short online reviews of a marked paper can be easier to fit than a travelling tutor's long session.

The syllabus facts do not change by city. ## What does a typical first month look like with IGCSE tutors in Delhi NCR?

Whichever city you are in, the first month with IGCSE tutors in Delhi NCR should follow the same arc: a diagnostic, a plan, a rhythm and a review. What differs is how the first session happens, not what it contains.

**Week one: the diagnostic.** The tutor asks for recent marked work, the syllabus code and the tier, then sets a short run of fresh questions. A Grade 10 student in a Noida society might do this at the dining table with the tutor beside her; a Greater Noida student might do it online with a phone on a stand over the page. In both, the student writes the working and explains aloud, and the tutor ends with a plain summary naming two or three priorities. If you receive a report that says "needs more practice", ask for something more specific.

**Week two: the first repair.** The tutor picks the highest-value gap. For a Mathematics student it might be algebraic manipulation that slows every later topic; for a Chemistry student, mole calculations; for an English student, planning a directed-writing answer in five minutes. The session has one modelled example, one shared attempt and one independent attempt, and finishes with a one-line takeaway in the error log.

**Week three: independent work and a first timed task.** The student does a short task between sessions, photographs the working and sends it. The tutor reviews it before the next lesson and starts the session from that evidence. This is the point at which a tutor who is working from a plan separates from one who is following the textbook.

**Week four: the review.** Repeat a short task from the first diagnostic. Compare the working, the accuracy and the time taken. Three outcomes are possible: clear movement (continue), partial movement (adjust the plan), or none (ask the tutor what changes, or request a different match). The review is the single habit that protects a family from drifting for a term.

### Two illustrative scenarios

Consider a Class 9 student in Faridabad who has just moved from a CBSE school. Her marks in the first IGCSE test are lower than she expected, and the cause is not her knowledge but the question style: she writes complete textbook answers where the command term asks for a short explanation, and she loses time on questions that reward a concise method. A tutor who has seen the board switch teaches her to read the verb first and to match depth to the mark allocation. Within a few weeks the marks follow, because the correction was about technique.

Now consider a Class 10 student in Gurgaon preparing for the IB Diploma next. His Extended Mathematics marks are strong but he finds the last questions on each paper unfamiliar. A tutor who works with him on multi-step problems, and who introduces Additional Mathematics topics early, is preparing him for the move to DP. These are illustrations, not real students, but the patterns are common in the NCR.

Three more points apply across the region. First, a summer afternoon and a foggy winter evening are both reasons to keep an online fallback for any home arrangement. Second, school holidays and local festivals shift the calendar, so agree in advance how missed sessions are made up. Third, siblings share tutors in some households, and where that works, it reduces the travel burden for the tutor without changing the quality for either child.

## Can siblings or friends share an IGCSE tutor?

Sometimes, and it depends on whether they study the same syllabus at the same stage. Two siblings in different grades benefit from one tutor only if the tutor teaches both subjects at both levels; a Grade 9 and a Grade 10 student need different content even in the same subject. Friends in the same class who follow the same board, tier and school calendar can share a small group, which lowers the cost per student, but the pace is then set between them, not by one student's gaps. For students with a specific weakness, one-to-one is usually better. For students who need structure and gain from explaining things to a peer, a pair can work well. Ask the tutor how they handle a pair, and watch for one student dominating while the other waits.

## When is a tutor not the answer?

A tutor is not the answer when the real problem is time, sleep or a school class that moves too fast and needs a conversation with the teacher. Adding a tutor to an overloaded week usually makes things worse. A tutor is also not the answer when the student has no wish to improve and sees the sessions as punishment. In both cases, fix the underlying issue first.

Some students do very well without outside help. A student who understands lessons, completes past papers under time and fixes their own errors does not need a tutor, and families should not feel pressured. If you want to run the process yourself, [personalised learning in IGCSE](/blog/personalized-learning-igcse-education/) shows how to use adaptive revision, spaced retrieval and an error log, and when outside help adds value.

No tutor can guarantee a grade. Where a grade depends on the student's starting level, the school's entries, attendance and independent practice, a promise is a warning.

## How do you compare a coaching centre with a home tutor in the NCR?

A coaching centre offers structure, a peer group and a set timetable at the cost of individual pace. A home tutor offers pace set by the student's gaps at the cost of peer energy. Neither is better in general.

| Factor | Coaching centre | Home or online one-to-one |
|---|---|---|
| Pace | Set by the batch | Set by the student |
| Feedback | General, from a distance | Specific, on the student's working |
| Travel | Student travels | None, or the tutor travels |
| Peer group | Yes | No |
| Flexibility | Fixed timetable | Easier to move around school |
| Fit for gaps | Weak | Strong |

For a student with a specific gap, one-to-one usually earns its place. For a student who needs structure and gains from peers, a good batch may suit. The maths-specific comparison is in [IGCSE maths home tutor vs coaching](/blog/igcse-maths-home-tutor-vs-coaching/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are there good IGCSE tutors in Delhi NCR outside Gurgaon?

Yes, but supply varies by area. Gurgaon has the densest pool, Delhi and Noida have established communities, and Greater Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad have fewer specialists nearby, which makes online and hybrid options more useful. The right test is not where the tutor lives but whether they teach your child's exact board, syllabus code and tier, and whether they diagnose before they teach.

### Should I choose home tuition or online for IGCSE in the NCR?

Choose by supply, journey and attention. Home suits students who focus better with a person in the room and live where tutors can arrive reliably. Online suits students who need a specialist who is not nearby or whose week is tight. Many families use both, with home sessions for maths and online for other subjects. A trial session shows which fits.

### Does the exam series change what kind of tutoring I need?

It changes the timeline, not the type. A student on the February/March Cambridge series, which is offered only in India, has an earlier deadline than one on May/June, so the plan must finish earlier. Pearson Edexcel series run in January and May/June. The school decides which series your child sits, so confirm it before you plan.

### Can an online tutor handle IGCSE science practicals?

Online tutoring can prepare students for the planning, data handling and evaluation parts of practical assessment, and for Paper 6 where the school uses the alternative to practical. It cannot replace laboratory time for Paper 5. If a student has missed practicals, tell the tutor and the school so gaps can be addressed. See the online IGCSE guide for details.

### My child is switching from CBSE to IGCSE. What changes?

Marking, mainly. IGCSE uses command terms and markschemes instead of a fixed answer key, and coursework and independent work carry more weight in some subjects. A student who has relied on memorised answers meets unfamiliar question styles. A tutor who has seen the switch can close the gap with a diagnostic and short practice cycles. Equivalence for Indian universities is a separate question to confirm with the institution.

### How many sessions a week does an IGCSE student need?

Start with one or two sessions a week per subject that needs support, each 45 to 60 minutes, with a short task between sessions. More hours without follow-up work rarely help. In the final weeks before a series, shorter, more frequent sessions on a marked paper are often more useful than a long weekly one.

### Can one tutor teach both maths and science?

Rarely at specialist level, and it depends on the tutor. Some tutors cover Mathematics and Physics well; fewer cover Mathematics and Biology. If your child needs strong support in both, two specialists are often better than one generalist. Ask the tutor which syllabus codes they teach, and judge by a trial lesson.

### What if the tutor lives too far for evening lessons?

Move those sessions online, or to a weekend slot, instead of letting late arrivals become normal. Long evening commutes make punctuality unreliable in much of the NCR. A tutor who lives or works close by is worth more than one with a stronger profile and a long journey. Online widens the pool without the commute.

### Will an IGCSE tutor promise a particular grade?

A responsible tutor will not. Grades depend on the student's starting level, attendance, independent practice and the school's assessment. A good tutor defines a process, shows evidence of progress and changes the plan when the evidence demands it. Treat any promise of a specific grade with caution.

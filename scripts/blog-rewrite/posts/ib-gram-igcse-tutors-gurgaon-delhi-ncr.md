## What Is IGCSE, and Which Version Is Your Child Taking?

IGCSE in India means Cambridge IGCSE (Cambridge International Education) or Pearson Edexcel International GCSE, usually a two-year course across Grades 9 and 10. It is often followed by the IB Diploma Programme (DP), Cambridge International AS and A Levels, or a move to CBSE or ISC in Class 11.

The two boards are not interchangeable, and a tutor should know which one a student sits.

| Feature | Cambridge IGCSE | Pearson Edexcel International GCSE |
|---|---|---|
| Exam series | February/March (India only), May/June, October/November | January and May/June |
| Grading | Most syllabuses A* to G; some have 9-1 versions under different codes | 9 to 1 |
| Tiers | Many subjects have Core and Extended | Mathematics A (4MA1) is Foundation or Higher |
| Maths code | 0580 | 4MA1 |
| Additional maths | 0606 Additional Mathematics | Further Pure Mathematics (4PM1) is the nearest equivalent |

Ask the school, not a tutor or a website, which board, syllabus code and series your child has been entered for. Schools decide which series they enter students for; a student cannot choose a series independently of the school. The February/March series exists specifically for India and lets Grade 10 results arrive in time for Indian school admissions to Class 11 and for IB DP or A Level entry in the same academic year.

## What Happens in the First Month of IGCSE Tuition?

The first month of IGCSE tuition in Gurgaon and Delhi NCR should produce a diagnosis, a plan and an early read on fit. It is not the time to expect a change in grades, though some students feel better about a subject within a few lessons.

| Week | What should happen | Warning sign |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tutor looks at recent school work and a timed section | Teaching starts at Chapter 1 regardless |
| 2 | First targeted lessons on the biggest gap | Homework help only |
| 3 | A timed section marked against the mark scheme | No record of errors |
| 4 | Review: what moved, what did not, what next | No change in plan |

Two practical tools make the month work. The first is an error log, where every lost mark is recorded with its cause (knowledge, method, accuracy or communication). The second is a plan written in a few lines that the family can read. If either is missing, ask. ## How Does Exam Timing Shape the Plan?

Exam timing decides what the tutor does in each term, so every tutor needs to know the series. Cambridge series run in February/March (offered only in India), May/June and October/November; Pearson Edexcel International GCSE series run in January and May/June.

A student entered for the February/March series has a shorter year than one entered for May/June, so revision begins sooner and mock results arrive earlier. A student heading for the IB DP after Grade 10 needs results in time for admission, which is part of why the India-only series exists. The Association of Indian Universities issues equivalence for international qualifications, and families should check current requirements with the university or board concerned rather than assume.

## What Are the Most Common Reasons IGCSE Students Lose Marks?

Across subjects the same four causes appear, and a diagnostic should find which one applies.

- **Command words.** Students answer "describe" with an explanation or "explain" with a description. The marks are for what the word asks.
- **Method and working.** Right answers with missing steps, or wrong answers with no method to earn partial credit.
- **Application.** Students know the content but cannot use it in an unfamiliar context.
- **Time.** Papers finished late, or the hardest questions attempted first.

## When Is a Tutor Not the Answer?

A tutor is not the answer when the student is already improving steadily, understands their own errors and has useful feedback from school. It is also not the answer when the real issue is workload, sleep or anxiety, which a tutor can reduce but not solve alone.

A fair test: can the student explain why marks were lost on the last paper? If yes, and the plan for the next one is specific, the family may need only a revision routine and perhaps a few sessions before the mocks. If not, a diagnostic from a tutor is a reasonable next step. Many families also find a tutor most useful for one subject, usually maths, and manage the rest alone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I find IGCSE tuition in Gurgaon and Delhi NCR?

Send IB Gram a request or book a free consultation with the board, grade, subject, school calendar, location and schedule. IB Gram then shortlists verified tutors who fit, subject to tutor availability.

Before the request, confirm the board, syllabus code and exam series with the school. These details decide which tutors are suitable.

### Does IB Gram cover both Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel?

Yes, requests can be for either Cambridge IGCSE or Pearson Edexcel International GCSE. The matching asks which board and syllabus code the student sits.

The two differ in papers, tiers, grading and series, so tell us the code, such as 0580 or 4MA1, rather than just "IGCSE maths". A tutor for one board is not automatically right for the other.

### Can my child try a lesson before we commit?

Yes. A demo or trial session can be arranged before the family commits, subject to tutor availability.

Use it to see how the tutor explains, whether they ask to see recent school work, and whether your child is comfortable. If the fit is wrong, you can ask for a different match.

### Are home tutors available in Noida, Faridabad and Delhi, or only Gurgaon?

IB Gram serves families across the NCR and beyond, and lessons can run at home, online or hybrid. Home lessons depend on tutor availability near you.

Online lessons widen the choice and suit areas where home tutors are scarce. Many families in Greater Noida and Faridabad start online and add home sessions later.

### Should my child take the Core or Extended tier?

The school decides entry, but your child's evidence should guide the discussion. Core grades top out at C and Extended reaches A*.

A tutor can give a view after a few weeks of work. Read [personalised home tuition for IGCSE students](/blog/personalized-home-tuition-igcse-students/) for how that conversation goes.

### How is IGCSE different from CBSE or ICSE?

IGCSE rewards command words, shown working and application more than memorised answers, and uses criterion-based marking against a mark scheme.

Students switching boards often need a month to adjust to how questions are worded and marked. Equivalence for Indian university admission is issued by the Association of Indian Universities, and families should confirm requirements with the institution.

### How many subjects should a tutor cover?

Usually one or two at a time. Maths is the most common choice because it supports the sciences and Economics.

Spreading tuition across many subjects leaves the student tired and the tutor thin. Review the mix each term, as needs change.

### What if my child is not improving after a month?

Ask the tutor for the error log and the plan. A month is enough to see whether lost marks are being diagnosed, even if grades have not moved.

If there is no log and no plan, ask for a different match. Poor fit is common and does not mean your child cannot improve.

### Will the tutor help with the IGCSE practical papers?

A tutor can teach the written skills for Paper 5 or Paper 6: planning, data handling, graphs and evaluation. A tutor cannot replace laboratory practice.

Which paper a student sits is chosen by the school, so ask the school first. See [IGCSE science home tuition](/blog/igcse-science-home-tuition-in-gurgaon/) for how this is handled.

### What does IGCSE tuition cost?

It varies with the tutor, subject, tier, frequency, format and travel. We do not publish a price range.

Ask any tutor what the fee covers and what you will see in the first month. A written diagnosis and a plan are signs of value; the hourly figure alone is not.

## How do IB and IGCSE tutoring needs differ?

IB tutoring is mostly about depth, independence and assessment skill across two programmes, while IGCSE tutoring is mostly about syllabus mastery, exam technique and speed within a defined specification. The tutor's job differs accordingly: with IB, the tutor works partly as a coach for how to think and write; with IGCSE, the tutor works partly as a drill instructor for question types.

Here is the contrast in one table.

| Dimension | IB (MYP and DP) | IGCSE (Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel) |
|---|---|---|
| Typical ages | MYP roughly 11 to 16; DP the final two years | Grades 9 and 10, usually a two-year course |
| What is assessed | Exams plus internal assessments that carry real weight | Mostly external papers, with some coursework in certain subjects |
| Marking style | Criteria and command terms; MYP criteria A to D, each 0 to 8 | Mark schemes and grade boundaries; Cambridge grades A* to G in most syllabuses |
| Common tutor tasks | Diagnosis, IA skills, extended writing, HL depth, planning | Syllabus coverage, past-paper technique, tier or paper choice, speed |
| Typical crunch points | DP1 transition, DP2 autumn deadlines, May session | February/March, May/June and October/November series, depending on the school |
| Subjects families ask about most | Mathematics AA and AI, sciences, Economics, English | Mathematics (0580, 4MA1), sciences, Economics, English, Computer Science |
| Tutor risk to avoid | Writing or editing a student's IA or EE | Teaching to a mark scheme without understanding |

:::stats The two boards in numbers
0-8 | Score per MYP criterion, A to D
1-7 | Grade scale for each DP subject
A*-G | Cambridge IGCSE grades in most syllabuses
3 | Cambridge exam series a year
:::

Two points deserve emphasis. First, in the IB the internal assessments are the student's own work, and a tutor who blurs that line harms the student. The IB's academic integrity policy allows a teacher to give feedback on one draft; it does not allow anyone, tutor or parent, to write, rewrite or edit the submission. Second, in IGCSE the series matters. Cambridge exam series run in February/March (a series offered only in India), May/June and October/November, and Pearson Edexcel International GCSE series run in January and May/June. Schools decide which series they enter students for, so a tutor's plan must start from the school's actual date, not a generic one.

## What does "best" mean when you search for IB and IGCSE tutors in Gurgaon?

"Best" means best for your child's board, level, subject, school calendar and temperament, not best on a list. A tutor who is excellent for a motivated Physics HL student at the top of the class may be wrong for a nervous Grade 9 student who needs patience, and no ranking can capture that.

Many "best tutors" pages are advertisements, and rankings of tutors cannot be verified. So rather than hand you a list, we suggest you use five tests, which any family can run in a week.

1. **The diagnostic test.** Does the tutor find out what the student can and cannot do before teaching? A tutor who begins teaching in the first minute has not diagnosed anything.
2. **The board-fit test.** Can the tutor describe, unprompted, how the board assesses the subject: command terms and criteria for IB, papers and tiers for IGCSE? Specific answers, not generalities.
3. **The explanation test.** Does the tutor ask before telling, and does the student do the next problem alone?
4. **The evidence test.** Is marked work returned? Is there a plan with a review point?
5. **The boundary test.** Does the tutor state what they will not do, such as writing coursework?

If a candidate passes all five, they are a good tutor for your child, whatever any list says. For the full checklist, including credentials, the demo lesson and red flags, see [how to choose an IB tutor in Gurgaon](/blog/how-to-choose-ib-tutor-gurgaon/).

## Which IB and IGCSE subjects do families in Gurgaon ask about most?

Families in Gurgaon most often ask about Mathematics (Analysis and Approaches or Applications and Interpretation in the IB; 0580 or 4MA1 in IGCSE), the three sciences, Economics, English and, more recently, Computer Science. The pattern follows where students lose marks: maths and sciences for problem solving, Economics and English for extended writing and evaluation.

A few notes by subject, so you can see what a tutor should know.

- **IB Mathematics.** DP Mathematics comes in two subjects, each at Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL): Analysis and Approaches (AA), which is algebraic and proof-heavy, and Applications and Interpretation (AI), which is modelling and statistics-heavy with technology used throughout. - **IGCSE Mathematics.** Cambridge 0580 has Core papers (1 and 3) and Extended papers (2 and 4); Pearson Edexcel 4MA1 is tiered Foundation or Higher. Additional Mathematics 0606 is often taken alongside Extended for students heading for IB Mathematics AA HL or A Level. If you are unsure which board a child's school follows for a particular subject, ask the school; tutors work from the school's choice.

## How do families in Gurgaon decide between home and online tutoring?

In Gurgaon, home tutoring suits students who focus best with a person in the room, in a quiet home, and a tutor who lives or works near the society; online tutoring suits students whose evenings are lost to traffic, who need a specialist not available nearby, or who have a packed week. Many families use both, home for term-time problem work and online for short revision blocks.

Gurgaon's practical details shape the choice. Many students live in gated societies where visitors must be registered at the gate, which adds friction for a new tutor. Weekday evening traffic on the main roads can turn a 30-minute trip into a longer one, and a tutor who arrives tired gives less. A student with swimming at 5 pm and a school bus at 7 am has a narrower window than the family may realise.

## What do IB and IGCSE tutors actually do in a session?

A good session has a recognisable shape whichever board it serves: it opens with last week's marked work, it works one priority with the student doing most of the writing, and it ends with a short set of questions for the days between. What differs is the content: for IB, command terms, criteria and extended answers; for IGCSE, question types, paper technique and speed.

Consider an IB DP1 student in Physics HL who loses marks on multi-step problems. A session might start by reviewing last week's error log, then work two mixed-topic problems where the student must choose the method, then a data question with uncertainty. The tutor stays quiet for most of it, and speaks mainly to ask "why".

Consider an IGCSE Grade 10 student in Extended Mathematics who is strong on algebra but slow in the calculator paper. A session might be a timed section from a past paper, a marked review of where time was lost, and a drill on the question types that cost the most. The goal is to raise accuracy under time pressure.

In both cases, the signal of a good session is that the student leaves knowing what to do next and why. The signal of a poor one is that the student has watched a competent adult solve problems.

## Is it worth hiring a one-to-one tutor at all?

It is worth it when there is a specific, repeated problem that school support has not fixed, and it is not worth it as a general safety net. A student who is on target, who acts on the school's feedback and who sleeps enough does not need a tutor, and adding one creates load.

## How should IB and IGCSE families approach the transition between boards?

Families moving from IGCSE to the IB Diploma Programme should plan the transition, because the habits that earned an A* often need to change. IGCSE rewards precise, bounded answers to a defined specification; DP rewards extended, structured and independent work, particularly in the internal assessments.

A student who scored well in IGCSE Extended Mathematics often finds DP1 harder than expected, because the questions are less like the ones practised and the pace is faster. Students heading for Mathematics AA HL commonly take IGCSE Additional Mathematics 0606 alongside Extended, which gives a head start in functions, series, vectors, differentiation and integration. The guide on [moving from IGCSE mathematics to IB DP](/blog/from-igcse-mathematics-to-ib-dp-building-a-strong-foundation/) covers the foundation in detail.

Students moving from CBSE, ICSE or a state board into IB or IGCSE meet criterion-referenced or command-term marking instead of a fixed answer key, coursework that carries real weight, and wider independent reading. Equivalence for Indian university admission is issued by the Association of Indian Universities, and requirements differ by university and course, so check with the institution concerned instead of relying on general advice. For the university side of DP choices, [IB university preparation](/blog/ib-university-preparation-guide/) explains how predicted grades and subject choices fit together.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who are the best IB and IGCSE tutors in Gurgaon?

There is no verifiable ranking, so the useful answer is how to find the best one for your child. The best tutor is the one who diagnoses your child's gaps, knows how the board assesses the subject, makes the student do the work, returns marked feedback and states what they will not do. Run a trial with two or three candidates, score them on the same tasks, and decide on evidence.

### What is the difference between an IB tutor and an IGCSE tutor?

An IB tutor works across programmes that include criteria-based marking, internal assessments and extended writing, while an IGCSE tutor works within a defined specification and paper-based exams. IB tutoring leans on coaching how to think and write; IGCSE tutoring leans on question types and exam technique. Some tutors teach both boards, but check that they can describe each board's assessment specifically.

### Can one tutor teach both IB and IGCSE?

Yes, some can, especially in subjects like mathematics, but ask for specifics. A tutor who teaches both should be able to describe the paper structure and marking style of each board separately, such as Cambridge 0580 papers and MYP criteria A to D. Be cautious if the answers blur together, because a tutor who treats the boards as the same is likely to teach to the wrong target.

### Is home tutoring or online tutoring better in Gurgaon?

Neither is better in general. Home tutoring suits students who focus better with a person in the room and live where a tutor can arrive without long travel; online suits students who lose time to traffic or need a specialist from elsewhere. Many families combine them. Try both in a trial if you are unsure, and judge by how much the student does in the session.

### How soon should we start tutoring for DP or Grade 10?

Start when a specific problem appears, not by default, and earlier is generally more useful than later because gaps compound. In the DP, early DP1 is often the best time to fix prerequisites; in IGCSE, Grade 9 gives room to repair foundations. Late-stage help is still valuable but is more about technique and practice than re-teaching. The guide on when to start IB tutoring takes each stage in turn.

### Do IB Gram tutors help with the IA and Extended Essay?

Tutors can teach the underlying skills but cannot write, rewrite or edit the student's work. The IB's academic integrity policy allows a teacher to give feedback on one draft, and no one else may edit the submission. Good help includes questioning the reasoning, explaining how criteria are applied and asking the student to reread their own work against the descriptors. Any offer beyond that is a warning sign.

### How does IB Gram match a family with a tutor?

A family sends a query or books a free consultation, and IB Gram asks about the board, programme, level, subject, school calendar, location and schedule. It shortlists tutors whose profiles are verified and who fit. A demo or trial session can be arranged before commitment, lessons run at home, online or hybrid, and the family can ask for a different match. Availability is subject to tutor availability.

### What if my area has few specialist tutors nearby?

Start online or hybrid. A specialist in another part of NCR can teach well online if the setup is right: a pen tablet or camera on the page, a shared whiteboard and a quiet space. A monthly home session can add the in-person element where it matters. This is common in Greater Noida, parts of Faridabad and outer areas, and the area guides explain how to make a hybrid plan work.

### Will a tutor promise a grade in IB or IGCSE?

No. No tutor can guarantee a grade, because the result depends on the student's work in the exam and, for the IB, on the internal assessment and school moderation. A tutor can raise the odds by fixing identifiable gaps and improving technique. Treat any grade promise as a reason to look elsewhere.

### What should I prepare before contacting a tutor or platform?

Prepare one sentence describing the problem, the board and level, the subject and school, the date of the next mock or exam series, and two recent marked pieces of work. With these, a tutor or platform can match you faster and a trial lesson can test the right thing. If the school has given feedback, share it. Vague requests produce vague matches.

## How do gate entry and visitor approval work for a home tutor?

Gate entry works best when the family pre-registers the tutor with the society's security before the first lesson, shares the tutor's name and a photo ID check with the gate, and sets a standing approval for fixed days. This removes the daily phone call and keeps the lesson from losing its first ten minutes.

A short checklist that families on the corridor can adapt:

1. **Ask the society office how to register a recurring visitor.** Many towers allow a standing approval through the resident app or the front desk. Rules vary and change, so ask rather than assume.
2. **Share the tutor's name, phone number and the lesson days** with security in advance.
3. **Agree a fixed arrival time** with the tutor, and a fall-back message if the gate approval is delayed.
4. **Confirm parking or drop-off.** Some towers have visitor parking limits in the evening. A tutor arriving by two-wheeler or cab has different needs.
5. **Plan for monsoon and festival days.** Gate queues and traffic change, and an agreed switch to an online lesson for that day saves a cancelled slot.

## When are evening slots realistic for DP students on Golf Course Road?

Realistic slots are usually weekday evenings after the school day and activities, in the late afternoon or early evening, plus weekend mornings. A one-hour or ninety-minute lesson that starts after the student has had a snack and a short break is far more productive than one squeezed in straight off the school bus. ### Three scheduling patterns that work

**The one-subject-a-day rhythm.** A student with two HL subjects needing support might have maths on Tuesday and Thursday evenings and a science on Saturday morning. The pattern keeps each lesson focused and gives a gap to complete homework between sessions.

**The weekend-heavy rhythm.** In weeks packed with weekday activities, a student may do two lessons on the weekend and a short online check-in midweek. Weekend tutoring is explored in [weekend IB tutoring in Gurgaon](/blog/weekend-ib-tutoring-gurgaon/).

**The hybrid rhythm.** Home on the two most important days, online on the third, so a monsoon downpour or a long school event does not break the week. How the formats compare is covered in [IB home tutor vs online tutor in Gurgaon](/blog/ib-home-tutor-vs-online-tutor-gurgaon/).

A caution on over-scheduling. A DP student who has five tutoring sessions a week plus school plus activities has no time left to practise alone, and practice alone is where learning is consolidated. For most students, one or two sessions a week per subject that needs help is the upper end, and a student who is doing well in a subject needs none.

### The three questions that tell you a tutor has real HL depth

1. **"Show me how you would diagnose my child's last paper."** A tutor with depth starts from the script and names two or three specific losses, not "weak in calculus".
2. **"What would you do in the first three sessions?"** The answer should include a diagnostic, a small target and an independent task.
3. **"Where do you stop?"** In internal assessments and the Extended Essay, a good tutor can name the integrity line: they may teach the skill and question the reasoning, but never write, rewrite or edit the student's work.

## What does a home study setup look like in a high-rise apartment?

A good home study setup is a quiet, lit, repeatable place with a table, a chair, a power point and no television or phone in line of sight. In a high-rise flat, the challenge is usually noise and shared space: a dining table that doubles as the family's evening space, or a bedroom with a bed that invites rest. A small change to the arrangement can shift the quality of every session.

Practical points for a tutor's visit:

- **A fixed spot.** The same table at the same time builds a habit. A flat with a study room can use it; otherwise the dining table cleared for the hour works.
- **Light and screen.** Natural light in the evening, a lamp, and a laptop or tablet with a stable connection for any shared resources or past papers.
- **Writing space.** Mathematics and the sciences need paper. A student who only types or watches loses the habit of writing a full solution.
- **Quiet for sixty minutes.** Agree with the household that the hour is protected: no calls, no television at volume. Many Gurgaon flats share walls and ceilings, so noise travels.
- **A visible supply of past papers and the formula booklet** so the tutor can pick a task at once.

Parents sometimes ask whether they should sit in. In early sessions it is reasonable to be nearby, and to check that the tutor's way of working suits the student. After that, a respectful distance lets the student concentrate, and a short update from the tutor at the end of the week serves better than watching.

## What should the first month with a tutor look like?

The first month should produce a diagnosis, a short plan, a visible change in how the student works, and a decision to continue or switch. If the first four sessions are general revision with no named priorities, the match probably needs a rethink. The first month is a test of fit, and a good tutor expects to be tested.

:::steps The first four weeks of a tutor match
Week 1 | Diagnostic from recent school work
Week 2 | Two named priorities, first drills
Week 3 | A repeat task to test change
Week 4 | Review: continue, adjust or switch
:::

1. **Week one: diagnose from the student's own work.** The tutor reads a recent test or assignment, listens to the student explain their method, and writes the two or three causes of lost marks in plain language.
2. **Week two: attack the largest cause.** A short, specific drill, and a task for the student to complete alone before the next session.
3. **Week three: re-test.** A comparable task checks whether the habit has changed.
4. **Week four: review with the family.** The tutor shares what changed, what did not, and the plan for the next month. The family may ask for a different match if the fit is wrong.

This is the principle behind IB Gram's offer of a demo or trial session: judge the method by watching it. Ask the tutor to teach one real problem, and watch whether the student does the thinking. ## How do coursework deadlines affect tutoring along Golf Course Road?

Coursework deadlines, the Internal Assessments (IAs), the Extended Essay (EE) and the Theory of Knowledge (TOK) essay and exhibition, create pressure points in DP1 and DP2 that no weekly plan can ignore. Many Gurgaon schools cluster deadlines in the same weeks as mocks, so a family should ask the school for the calendar early and plan tutoring around it.

Every IB internal assessment is the student's own work. The IB's academic integrity policy allows a teacher to give feedback on one draft; it does not allow anyone, tutor or parent, to write, rewrite or edit the student's submission. A tutor may teach the underlying skill, question the reasoning, point at a criterion and ask the student to reread their own paragraph against it. The school submits the work and runs it through similarity checking, and malpractice findings are handled by the IB, not the school alone. For more on the process, read the [IB IA guide](/blog/ib-internal-assessment-guide/) and the [Extended Essay guide](/blog/how-to-write-a-perfect-ib-extended-essay-ee/). ## How do you tell a strong tutor from a convenient one among IB tutors near Golf Course Road?

When families compare IB tutors near Golf Course Road, a convenient tutor lives close and has open slots. A strong tutor diagnoses, teaches the assessment, sets independent work and knows the integrity limits. Convenience is a useful tie-breaker but a poor primary filter, especially for HL subjects, where a mismatch costs months. Compare three tutors on evidence, not on distance.

Ask for the following, and weigh the answers:

- **A description of the diagnostic**, not a promise of results.
- **A sample of how the tutor marks**: the tutor should be able to explain how marks are awarded against the IB's markscheme or criteria.
- **A plan for independence**: what does the student do between sessions?
- **A position on integrity**: where does the tutor stop on IA, EE and TOK?
- **A trial lesson** in the student's own subject, with the student doing most of the work.

No tutor can guarantee a grade. Be wary of any profile or conversation that does.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can an IB tutor come to my apartment near Golf Course Road?

Yes, subject to tutor availability, home lessons are possible in the towers along the corridor. The practical points are pre-registering the tutor with society security, agreeing a fixed arrival time and checking parking or drop-off. ### What time of day works best for IB lessons?

Early evening after the student has rested usually works best, with weekend mornings for longer sessions. A lesson straight after school or late at night is less productive. Weekday traffic can be heavy on and around the road, so tutors who travel need a buffer. If the evenings are crowded, one home lesson plus one online lesson a week is a common compromise.

### How many lessons a week does a DP student need?

One or two sessions a week for a subject that needs help is typical, with fewer for subjects that are going well. More than that can crowd out the independent practice that makes lessons stick. The right number depends on the gap, the subject and the calendar, and it can rise before mocks. A good tutor will tell you if more sessions would not help.

### Is a home tutor better than an online tutor for HL subjects?

Neither is better in general. Home suits students who focus better away from screens and subjects with heavy handwriting and diagrams. Online suits busy evenings, specialist availability and flexibility. For HL maths and sciences, a digital pen or camera on paper makes online workable. Many families do hybrid. The format matters less than whether the tutor diagnoses and sets independent work.

### Can a tutor help with my child's Internal Assessment or Extended Essay?

A tutor can teach the skills and question the reasoning, but cannot write, rewrite or edit the work. IB integrity rules require the submission to be the student's own, and the school's similarity checking looks for outside writing. Useful help includes planning, understanding criteria and reviewing the student's own paragraph against them. If you are unsure whether help has crossed a line, ask the DP coordinator.

### How do I know if the tutor is a good match after the first lesson?

Check whether the tutor diagnosed from your child's own work, named specific priorities and gave a task to do alone. Watch whether your child did most of the thinking. A single lesson cannot prove results, but it shows method. If the first session was general revision, ask for a different approach or request a different match.

### What if our society has strict visitor rules?

Ask the society office how to register a recurring visitor and share the tutor's details in advance. Many towers have an app or front-desk approval, but procedures vary and change. If entry is a recurring obstacle, an online lesson on the worst days keeps the schedule intact. Raise the issue early, not after the third lesson is late.

### Should my child start tutoring in DP1 or wait for DP2?

Start when a gap appears, not when a crisis arrives. DP1 is the cheapest time to repair foundations, because the pace is lower and the exam is two years away. Waiting for DP2 mocks often means fixing a gap under pressure. If DP1 is going well, there is no need to start early.

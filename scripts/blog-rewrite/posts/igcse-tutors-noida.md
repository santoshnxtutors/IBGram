It can mean the Cambridge IGCSE from Cambridge International Education, or the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE, and the two differ in paper structure, tiers, grading scale and even exam months. A tutor who is excellent for one can be a poor fit for the other, because the habits that collect marks are written into the papers.

## Which IGCSE does your Noida school actually run?

Ask the school two questions in writing: which awarding body (Cambridge International Education or Pearson Edexcel), and which syllabus code for each subject. Cambridge codes look like 0580 for Mathematics and 0610, 0620 and 0625 for Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Edexcel codes look like 4MA1 for Mathematics A and 4BI1, 4CH1 and 4PH1 for the sciences. The school's subject teacher can answer in a line.

The reason this matters is practical. Past papers, mark schemes and examiner reports are published per board and per code, so a tutor can only drill the right ones if they know the code. A student who has been practising Cambridge Extended papers while the school enters them for Edexcel Higher will sit a differently structured exam in a few months.

If the school is unsure or says "IGCSE" without detail, ask for the entry form or the syllabus page the school gives to students. Some schools run Cambridge for most subjects and Edexcel for one or two, so confirm subject by subject.

## What is the difference between Cambridge IGCSE and Pearson Edexcel International GCSE?

Both are international qualifications usually taken over Grades 9 and 10, and both are recognised by schools and universities worldwide. The differences that affect tutoring are paper structure, tiering, grading scale and exam series. IGCSE in India means Cambridge IGCSE or Pearson Edexcel International GCSE, usually a two-year course, often followed by the IB Diploma Programme, Cambridge International AS and A Levels, or a move to CBSE or ISC in Class 11.

| Feature | Cambridge IGCSE | Pearson Edexcel International GCSE |
|---|---|---|
| Awarding body | Cambridge International Education | Pearson Edexcel |
| Maths syllabus | 0580, Core and Extended tiers | Mathematics A (4MA1), Foundation and Higher tiers |
| Maths papers | Core: Paper 1 and 3; Extended: Paper 2 and 4 | Two calculator papers per tier |
| Grading | Most syllabuses A* to G; some 9-1 versions under different codes | 9 to 1 |
| Sciences | Papers 1 to 6 with Core or Extended and a practical component | Untiered, Paper 1 and Paper 2 per separate science |
| Exam series | February/March (India only), May/June, October/November | January and May/June |
| Practical assessment | Paper 5 or Paper 6, chosen by the school | Skills assessed inside the written papers |

The table shows why a generic IGCSE tutor can be a weak match. A Cambridge science tutor will coach multiple-choice, theory and a practical or alternative-to-practical paper. An Edexcel science tutor will prepare for two written papers with practical skills woven in. In maths, the Cambridge non-calculator paper is a distinct experience that Edexcel students do not have in the same form.

## Mathematics: where the two boards diverge most

Mathematics is where most Noida families look first for a tutor, and where the board matters most.

Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 (from the 2025 exams): Core candidates sit Paper 1 (non-calculator) and Paper 3 (calculator); Extended candidates sit Paper 2 (non-calculator) and Paper 4 (calculator). Core tops out at grade C, and Extended reaches A*. Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) is tiered Foundation or Higher, with two calculator papers per tier and grades 9 to 1.

**What this means for a tutor.** A Cambridge Extended student needs hand fluency for Paper 2, where there is no calculator and exact values, surds, fractions and algebraic manipulation are visible. An Edexcel Higher student works with a calculator on both papers, so the weight shifts to modelling, multi-step reasoning and calculator efficiency. The content overlaps heavily, but practice sets differ, and the mark schemes reward slightly different presentation.

Students who plan to take IB Mathematics AA HL or A Level Mathematics usually add Additional Mathematics. Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics 0606 has no tiers, two written papers with a calculator allowed, and is graded A* to E. Pearson Edexcel's nearest equivalent is International GCSE Further Pure Mathematics (4PM1). A tutor who knows only one board's version cannot guide a student well on the other.

## Sciences: tiers, practicals and who does what

The sciences differ more than most parents expect.

Cambridge IGCSE sciences (syllabuses examined from 2023) have Paper 1 multiple choice (Core) or Paper 2 multiple choice (Extended), Paper 3 theory (Core) or Paper 4 theory (Extended), and practical assessment through Paper 5 (practical test) or Paper 6 (alternative to practical), chosen by the school. Weighting: multiple choice 30%, theory 50%, practical 20%. Pearson Edexcel International GCSE sciences are untiered, with Paper 1 and Paper 2 for each separate science and practical skills assessed inside the written papers.

| Subject | Cambridge code | Pearson Edexcel code |
|---|---|---|
| Biology | 0610 | 4BI1 |
| Chemistry | 0620 | 4CH1 |
| Physics | 0625 | 4PH1 |
| Mathematics | 0580 | 4MA1 |
| Additional or Further Maths | 0606 | 4PM1 |
| Computer Science | 0478 | 4CP0 |
| Economics | 0455 | 4EC1 |
| Business | 0450 | 4BS1 |

**What this means for a tutor.** For Cambridge, a science tutor must be able to prepare a student for Paper 6, the alternative to practical, in which students answer written questions about experiments they may never have run. This is a recognised skill: reading apparatus diagrams, identifying variables, plotting graphs from tables and suggesting improvements. For Edexcel, practical skills show up as questions inside the main papers, so there is no separate paper to rehearse, but the same skills matter.

Cambridge Biology 0610 covers topics from characteristics and classification of living organisms through enzymes, transport in plants and animals, and respiration to inheritance, biotechnology and genetic modification. Physics 0625 covers motion, forces and energy, thermal physics, waves, electricity and magnetism, nuclear physics and space physics, and students must recall and rearrange the equations in the syllabus. Chemistry 0620 includes stoichiometry (including the mole), electrochemistry, chemical energetics and organic chemistry. A tutor should be able to say which of these topics cost students marks, not just list them. The [guide to why students struggle in IGCSE Science](/blog/igcse-science-tutoring-guide/) explains the common causes.

## English, economics, business and computer science

Outside maths and science, the boards also differ by subject, and a Noida family should confirm the code before booking.

English at IGCSE is several different syllabuses. Cambridge has First Language English 0500 (a reading paper, then directed writing and composition or a coursework portfolio), English as a Second Language 0510 and 0511 (reading and writing plus listening; 0511 counts speaking into the grade, 0510 reports speaking separately), and Literature in English 0475. Pearson Edexcel offers International GCSE English Language A (4EA1), English Language B (4EB1) and English as a Second Language (4ES1). A tutor must know which of these the student is studying, because First Language and Second Language call for very different coaching.

Cambridge IGCSE Economics 0455 has Paper 1 multiple choice (30%) and Paper 2 structured questions with data (70%). Edexcel (4EC1) has Paper 1 on microeconomics and business economics and Paper 2 on macroeconomics and the global economy. Cambridge Business Studies 0450 has Paper 1 short answer and data response and Paper 2 case study, 50% each, and Edexcel (4BS1) separates Investigating small businesses from Investigating large businesses. Cambridge Computer Science 0478 has Paper 1 on computer systems and Paper 2 on algorithms, programming and logic; Edexcel (4CP0) pairs a written paper with an on-screen practical programming paper in Python.

## How does a tutor's board knowledge change the lessons?

A board-aware tutor uses the right past papers, teaches the right command words, and knows how marks are structured in that specific mark scheme. That affects the first four weeks more than any other factor, because the early lessons set the habits.

Three concrete differences show up in practice.

**Past papers and mark schemes.** A Cambridge tutor uses Cambridge past papers and examiner reports by code and series. An Edexcel tutor uses Edexcel papers and mark schemes. Mixing them produces practice that does not match the exam, and a student marked against the wrong scheme learns the wrong presentation.

**Command words and answer format.** Both boards use command words such as state, describe, explain and evaluate, but the mark allocation and the expected length vary by subject and paper. A tutor who has marked or studied scripts from the right board can tell the student exactly how many points a three-mark explanation needs.

**Tier decisions.** For Cambridge, Core versus Extended and for Edexcel, Foundation versus Higher are decided early and shape what the tutor should teach. A tutor who ignores the tier wastes the student's time on topics that are not examined or skips ones that are.

## Series timing: when Noida students actually sit the exams

Cambridge exam series run in February/March (a series offered only in India), May/June and October/November; Pearson Edexcel International GCSE series run in January and May/June. Schools decide which series they enter students for, and a student cannot choose a series independently of the school.

The February/March Cambridge series exists specifically for India and lets Grade 10 results arrive in time for Indian school admissions to Class 11 and for IB Diploma Programme or A Level entry in the same academic year. A Noida family whose school enters the February/March series has a much shorter run into the exam than one entered for May/June, and the revision plan should reflect that.

| Series | Board | What it means for a tutor's plan |
|---|---|---|
| January | Edexcel | Very early peak; the final term starts almost with the school year |
| February/March | Cambridge (India) | Results arrive before Class 11 admissions; compressed calendar |
| May/June | Both | The widest window; the common choice in many schools |
| October/November | Cambridge | Used by some schools and for retakes |

Ask the school which series applies to your child and when the school's own mock examinations are. Share both dates with the tutor on the first day.

## Who needs IGCSE tutors in Noida, and who does not?

Many Noida students do well with school support alone, so IGCSE tutors in Noida are not a default. A tutor adds most in four situations.

- **The student is new to IGCSE.** Students moving in from CBSE or ICSE meet command-term marking, coursework and independent research for the first time. The first term is when a tutor helps most. ## Home, online or hybrid: what IGCSE tutors in Noida can offer

Mode is a logistics decision, and Noida has a few specific logistics worth planning for.

**Gated societies.** Many families in the sector clusters and along the Noida Expressway live in large gated societies with visitor registration at the gate. A home tutor will need a pre-registered entry or a call from the gate, and a family that forgets this loses the first ten minutes of a session. Give the tutor the tower, the flat number and the gate protocol in advance.

**Weekday evenings.** Evening traffic on the main roads can make a home visit at 5 pm slower than at 7 pm. Ask the tutor what slot they can reliably meet, and do not schedule back-to-back across two societies.

**Study space.** A table, a clear wall or board, and a quiet room matter more than the tutor's travel. A younger sibling in the same room is a common reason home sessions drift.

**When online is better.** For a student with a packed evening, a tutor in a hard-to-reach cluster, or a subject where the best match is across the city, online sessions with a pen tablet and shared whiteboard work well. **Hybrid.** Many students use home sessions in the main teaching terms and online sessions in the weeks before exams, when flexibility matters most.

## How to judge a tutor in a trial session

A trial session is the cheapest way to check board fit. Whatever the subject, you can test four things in 45 minutes.

1. **Ask which syllabus code they are working to.** A good tutor names it immediately and says which tier.
2. **Ask them to show a past paper question and its mark scheme.** They should explain where the marks sit and how a typical student loses them.
3. **Watch how they handle a student's mistake.** The tutor should ask the student to explain, not just correct.
4. **Check what the student leaves with.** A specific habit to change and a short task to complete before next time.

**Student A** is in Grade 9 and her school enters students for Cambridge IGCSE. She is in the Extended maths tier, and the school will enter the February/March series. His family needs a tutor who knows 4MA1 and the Edexcel science papers. ## What a good first month looks like

The first month of tutoring is where a family can judge value. A sensible shape, for either board, is below.

| Week | Focus | What the family should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnostic: board, code, tier, recent papers | A written summary of two or three priorities |
| 2 | Repair the highest-impact gap | A short practice set and a first error log |
| 3 | Topic work with mixed questions | Fewer repeated errors in marked work |
| 4 | First timed section against the mark scheme | A review of marks lost and a plan for next month |

By the end of the month, the student should be able to say what they are changing and why. If they cannot, ask the tutor to explain the plan. ## Moving from another board into IGCSE in Noida

Students who join an IGCSE school from CBSE, ICSE or a state board face a set of predictable adjustments. They meet criterion-referenced or command-term marking instead of a fixed answer key, coursework and internal assessment that carry real weight, wider reading and independent research, and less emphasis on memorised derivations. Equivalence for Indian university admission is issued by the Association of Indian Universities, and requirements differ by university and by course, so families should confirm with the institution rather than rely on general advice.

A tutor helps most in this transition by teaching how answers are marked, not by reteaching content the student already knows. Students who have been strong in CBSE maths often find IGCSE questions wordier and more applied, and students strong in CBSE science sometimes discover that explanation answers earn more than recall. A short block of sessions in the first term often does more than a long engagement later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my school follows Cambridge or Edexcel?

Ask the subject teacher or the school office for the awarding body and the syllabus code for each subject. Cambridge codes look like 0580 or 0620, and Edexcel codes look like 4MA1 or 4CH1. Check the school's entry form or the syllabus document given to students. Some schools use different boards for different subjects, so confirm each one before you share anything with a tutor.

### Can a Cambridge IGCSE tutor teach Pearson Edexcel?

Often partly, but not reliably. The content overlaps a lot, but papers, tiers, grading and mark schemes differ. A tutor who has coached both boards can adapt, while one who knows only Cambridge may drill the wrong papers. Ask the tutor directly which board they have taught, which codes, and to explain how a mark scheme in your board is structured.

### Is the grading scale different between the boards?

Yes. Cambridge grades most syllabuses A* to G, and some syllabuses have 9-1 versions under different codes. Pearson Edexcel International GCSE grades 9 to 1. For Cambridge maths, Core tops out at grade C and Extended reaches A*. Confirm the scale for your own syllabus code, because it affects how targets are described and how a tutor sets goals.

### Do I need a tutor who lives in my sector in Noida?

Not necessarily. For home sessions, a tutor who can reliably reach your society at your chosen time matters more than the sector name. For online sessions, location is irrelevant, and the best subject match can come from anywhere. Many families use home lessons in term and online sessions before exams. Subject to tutor availability, IB Gram shortlists tutors by board, subject, level, location and schedule.

### Which is better for my child, home or online tuition?

Neither is better in general. Home suits younger students, subjects that need side-by-side paper work and families who prefer an in-person routine. Online suits packed schedules, hard-to-reach locations and last-minute exam preparation. A hybrid arrangement is common. Judge by the student's attention, the quality of the tutor's whiteboard setup and whether work is actually being done between sessions.

### My child is entered for the February/March series. Does that change the plan?

Yes, because the calendar is compressed. The February/March Cambridge series is offered only in India and produces results in time for Class 11 and IB or A Level admissions. Revision should start earlier, mock dates matter more, and any tutoring should begin well before the final term. Share the school's mock and exam dates with the tutor on the first day.

### How many sessions a week does an IGCSE student need?

For most defined problems, one session a week plus the student's own practice is enough. Two sessions suit short windows such as the weeks before mocks, and only if the student completes the work set. Frequency matters less than whether the student acts on each session before the next. Review after four to six sessions and adjust.

### Should I hire separate tutors for maths and science?

Often yes, if both need help, because subject depth is hard to find in one person. A single tutor can sometimes cover maths and a science for a strong student, but the combination rarely works if both need heavy support. The question of one tutor or two is covered in the guide to [IGCSE maths and science tutors in Gurgaon](/blog/top-igcse-tutors-in-gurgaon-for-maths-science/), and the logic is the same.

### What if the first tutor is not a good fit?

Say so, early, and ask for a different match. Look at the evidence first: whether the student's marked work is changing and whether the tutor adapts to feedback. IB Gram can arrange a trial session before you commit and shortlist another tutor if the first is not right, subject to availability. A first mismatch is common and not a reason to give up on tutoring.

### Will a tutor promise a particular grade?

No. No tutor can guarantee a grade. Progress depends on the starting point, the time available, the student's practice and the exam. A tutor can diagnose where marks are lost, build a plan, teach the skills and hold the routine. Be cautious of anyone who promises a specific result, and judge by whether marked work improves over a few weeks.

## What Does Good Home Tuition in Gurgaon Actually Involve?

Good home tuition in Gurgaon involves one-to-one lessons, at the family's home, delivered by a tutor who knows the student's board and programme, working from the student's own marked work to a written plan, with regular feedback to parents and a review at fixed points. The location is the least important part. The matching, the plan and the review decide whether it works.

For IB students, that means a tutor who can speak the language of the International Baccalaureate (IB): Middle Years Programme (MYP) criteria scored 0 to 8 across four criteria per subject, or Diploma Programme (DP) papers, Internal Assessments (IA), Theory of Knowledge (TOK), the Extended Essay (EE) and Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS). For IGCSE students, that means a tutor who knows the difference between Cambridge IGCSE and Pearson Edexcel International GCSE, between Core and Extended tiers, and between exam series. A tutor who teaches "Class 10 maths" in general is not the same as one who can talk through 0580 Paper 4 or 4MA1 Paper 2.

It also means being honest about what home tuition cannot do. It cannot supply motivation the student lacks, replace school teaching, or compress two years of missed foundations into two weeks. Those limits are covered in each section below.

## Step One: The Query and the Free Consultation

A family starts by sending a query or booking a free consultation. This is a conversation, not a sales pitch, and its purpose is to gather the information a tutor needs to be useful. IB Gram asks about the following, and families get a better shortlist when they answer specifically.

1. **Board and programme.** IB (MYP or DP), Cambridge IGCSE or Pearson Edexcel International GCSE.
2. **Level.** The year or grade, and for the DP, Higher Level (HL) or Standard Level (SL). For IGCSE, Core or Extended, Foundation or Higher.
3. **Subject.** Not "maths" but, for example, "MYP 4 extended maths", "DP Math AA HL" or "IGCSE Chemistry 0620".
4. **School calendar.** Term dates, internal examination weeks, IA and EE deadlines, mock dates. IB schools in India set their own calendars and the ordering of units varies.
5. **Location.** The sector, society or locality, since it affects gate access, travel and whether a home or online session is realistic.
6. **Schedule.** The weekday evenings and weekend slots that are genuinely free, after school, sport, music and CAS.
7. **What is going wrong or what is wanted.** A recent assessment result, a gap, a goal, or simply "we want to prepare early".

The seventh is the one parents most often leave out, and the one that most improves a match. A student who lost marks in criterion C of MYP Mathematics needs a different tutor from one who lost marks in algebra. "We want a good maths tutor" cannot distinguish them.

### What to have ready

Before the call, collect the last two or three returned assessments, the school calendar and a rough idea of the weekly time available. It saves a week of back and forth, and it is the same material the tutor will need for a diagnostic later. ## Step Two: How a Shortlist Is Built

Once IB Gram has the information, it shortlists tutors whose profiles are verified and who fit the request. A good shortlist is short, with a reason for each name on it. If a family receives a long list of names with no explanation of fit, the platform has handed the work back to the parent.

Fit is judged on several things at once, and no single factor wins:

- **Programme and level knowledge.** MYP, DP and IGCSE are different worlds. A tutor strong in DP Physics HL is not automatically strong in MYP Sciences.
- **Subject depth.** For Mathematics, whether the tutor knows the difference between Analysis and Approaches (AA) and Applications and Interpretation (AI), and what each assesses.
- **Mode.** Whether the tutor teaches at home in the family's part of Gurgaon, online, or both.
- **Schedule.** Whether the tutor's real availability overlaps with the family's.
- **Student profile.** A student who is anxious may do better with a patient, slower tutor. A student who is bright but bored may need pace and challenge.

Availability is subject to tutor availability, and that is not a disclaimer to skip over. Good tutors fill their weekday evenings first, and families who ask in August for a slot that begins in September have a better choice than those who ask in October.

### What "verified" should mean to you

IB Gram shortlists tutors whose profiles are verified. A family should treat that as a starting point, not a replacement for its own checks. Sensible additional steps, which any family can take with any tutor from any source, include:

- Asking the tutor to describe, in specific terms, what they have taught in this programme and subject.
- Asking for the tutor's identification and sharing it with the building gate and a trusted adult before the first home visit.
- Asking how the tutor handles Internal Assessments within the IB's academic integrity rules (see below).
- Speaking to the tutor directly before the trial, even for five minutes.

Verification tells you a profile has been checked. It does not tell you the tutor will suit your child. The trial session is where fit shows.

## Step Three: The Trial Session

A demo or trial session can be arranged before the family commits. It is the most important step in the process, and families who skip the preparation for it waste it.

### What a useful trial looks like

A trial is not a lesson on the next chapter. It should show you three things: how the tutor diagnoses, how the student responds, and how the tutor communicates with you afterwards.

1. **The tutor asks for the student's marked work first,** and spends the opening minutes reading it.
2. **The student does something live,** such as an unfamiliar question pitched slightly above the current level, while the tutor watches and says little.
3. **The tutor explains one thing, briefly,** and the student tries again. This shows the tutor's explanation style and whether the student can take it in.
4. **The tutor says what they would do next.** Two or three priorities, in order, with a reason for each.
5. **A short written note follows,** to the parent, the same day.

### What to watch as a parent

Watch the student's face more than the tutor's. Are they thinking, or waiting? Do they ask a question? Do they relax within ten minutes? After the session, ask the student two things: "did that feel useful?" and "would you want to do that every week?". A tutor who is knowledgeable but makes the student feel small is not a fit, and the student's answer will show it.

### Questions to ask the tutor in the trial

- How will you decide what to teach in the first month?
- What will you send me after each session?
- What will you do about the IA, and what will you not do?
- What would make you recommend we stop?

A family that asks these will learn more about the tutor in ten minutes than from any list of credentials. ## Step Four: Lessons at Home, Online or Hybrid

After the trial, if the family wants to continue, lessons begin in whichever mode suits. IB Gram arranges lessons at home, online or hybrid, and the family can switch as circumstances change.

### Home

Home sessions suit paper-based work, handwriting-heavy subjects and younger students who benefit from a physical presence. In Gurgaon, three practical points shape them. Most societies require the visitor to be registered at the gate, so the family should arrange access in advance. Weekday evening traffic affects the tutor's arrival, so plan a start time with a buffer. And the study space matters: a quiet table beats a bedroom floor.

Online sessions suit families further from the tutor's route, students in exam season who need extra flexibility, and subjects where a shared whiteboard and a graphic calculator on screen work as well as paper. ## Step Five: Review and the Right to Change

Tutoring is not a contract to be endured. IB Gram's process includes a review point, and a family can ask for a different match if the first one does not fit. This is not a failure of the process. A match is a hypothesis, and the first month is how a family tests it.

:::timeline A typical first month
Week 1 | Trial, diagnostic and written plan
Weeks 2-3 | Regular sessions; short update after each
Week 4 | Review: what moved, what did not
Week 5 | Keep, adjust the plan, or request a different match
:::

### A four-question review

At the end of the first month, a family can run a simple review with the tutor.

1. **Has the evidence moved?** Evidence means a school assessment, a criterion mark or a timed paper, not an impression.
2. **Have the habits moved?** Even if marks have not changed, are the errors different in kind?
3. **Is the student more independent?** Starting problems without prompting is a good sign.
4. **Is the load sustainable?** Sleep, mood and the rest of the timetable matter.

If the answers are no, no, no, no after four to six weeks of regular sessions, the diagnosis, the fit or the plan needs to change. More hours of the same thing will not.

## How Do You Judge the Best Home Tuition in Gurgaon for Your Child?

The best home tuition in Gurgaon for your child is the arrangement that passes a handful of checks for that child, not a ranking. No platform, IB Gram included, can truthfully claim to be best for every student, and any page that says so is selling, not informing. Use the tests below on every option you consider.

1. **Programme fit.** Can the provider show knowledge of your child's actual programme, with specifics such as MYP criteria, DP papers or IGCSE paper codes?
2. **Diagnostic.** Is there a first step that looks at your child's marked work, or does teaching begin at the next chapter?
3. **A written plan.** Priorities, in order, with a review date.
4. **Communication.** Short updates after sessions, a monthly note and an answer within a reasonable time.
5. **Exit.** A clear, easy way to change tutor or stop without awkwardness.
6. **Integrity.** An unprompted statement that they will not write or edit assessed work.
7. **Honesty about limits.** A willingness to say when tutoring is not the answer.

A provider that passes all seven is worth a trial. One that passes four is a risk. One that promises a grade should be left alone.

## What the First Four Weeks Should Look Like

A family can use this as a checklist. If most items are not happening by the end of week four, raise it.

1. **Before the first session,** the tutor has seen recent marked work and the school calendar.
2. **In week one,** there is a trial or first session with a diagnostic element, and a written plan follows within days.
3. **By week two,** a rhythm is set: a fixed day and time, a make-up routine and an update format.
4. **By week three,** the student is doing small tasks between sessions, and an error log or equivalent exists.
5. **At week four,** a review takes place, with a candid statement from the tutor of what has and has not moved.

For IB students the plan will refer to criteria or components; for IGCSE students, to paper types and tiers. The family does not need to understand the content to see whether these steps happened.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does IB Gram find a home tutor in Gurgaon?

A family sends a query or books a free consultation. IB Gram asks about board, programme, level, subject, school calendar, location and schedule, then shortlists tutors whose profiles are verified and who fit. A demo or trial session can be arranged before the family commits. Availability is subject to tutor availability, so earlier enquiries usually have a wider choice.

### Is the consultation really free, and do I have to continue?

The consultation is free, and a trial session is arranged before commitment. A family is not obliged to continue after the trial. The purpose of the trial is to let the family and student judge fit before deciding. If it does not fit, the family can ask for a different match or step away.

### What does "verified" mean for a tutor profile?

It means IB Gram has checked the profile before shortlisting. It does not guarantee the tutor will suit your child, and families should still run their own sensible checks: ask about experience in the specific programme, share the tutor's identification with the building gate, and use the trial session to judge fit. Verification is a starting point, not a substitute for judgement.

### Can I get a home tutor for both IB and IGCSE?

Yes, IB Gram works with both IB (MYP and DP) and IGCSE (Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel). The shortlist depends on the programme, level and subject, since a tutor who is strong in DP Physics HL is not automatically a fit for IGCSE Physics 0625. Say which board and syllabus code the school uses in the first query, so the match is accurate.

### What if the first tutor is not a good fit?

Ask for a different match. A family can request this at any time, and the first month review is the natural moment. Share what did and did not work, such as pace, communication or subject depth, so the next shortlist improves. Give the new tutor the previous plan and the student's marked work so the diagnostic does not restart from nothing.

### Can IB Gram promise a particular grade?

No. No tutor or platform can guarantee a grade, and an honest provider will say so. Grades depend on the student's work, the school's teaching, the examiners' marking and the grade boundaries each session. What a good process can do is make the effort better aimed: a diagnostic, a plan, regular feedback and an early review. Judge by evidence, not promises.

### Can the tutor help with my child's Internal Assessment or Extended Essay?

A tutor can teach the skills behind the work and question the student's reasoning, but cannot write, rewrite or edit the submission. The IB's academic integrity policy allows a teacher to give feedback on one draft, and malpractice findings are handled by the IB. Any tutor who offers to write or "polish" the IA or EE puts the student's diploma at risk.

### Is home tuition better than online tuition in Gurgaon?

Neither is better in itself. Home suits paper-based work, handwriting and younger students, though gate access and evening traffic affect it. Online suits flexible scheduling, recorded review and exam-season intensity. Many students use both. The quality of the diagnostic, plan and review matters more than the mode, and the family can change modes as needs change.

### How soon can lessons start?

It depends on tutor availability in your subject, level, area and time window. Families who enquire several weeks before they need the first session usually have more options than those who need a start within days. Evening slots on weekdays fill earliest in the academic year, and weekend slots tend to be more available.

### Does my child need home tuition at all?

Not always. If assessments show a steady trajectory and the student can name their own error patterns, extra tuition may add load without adding value. A short block before mock exams, or a single-topic fix, can be enough. A good provider will tell you when the answer is no, and a free consultation is a reasonable way to find out.

### What should I prepare before contacting IB Gram?

Collect the last two or three marked assessments, the school calendar with mock dates and deadlines, and a list of the evenings and weekend slots genuinely free. Know the board, programme, level and subject, and write one sentence about what is going wrong or what you want. That lets the shortlist be specific and the trial session start from evidence.

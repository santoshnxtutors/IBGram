## What Should You Look For in IB Tutors in Faridabad?

You should look for four things, in this order: subject and level depth, familiarity with how the IB assesses, an honest diagnostic and a working schedule that suits a Faridabad household. A tutor who can teach DP Physics Higher Level (HL) in a way that matches how papers are marked is worth more than a tutor with a long list of subjects and no depth in any of them. The label "IB tutor" alone tells you very little; the evidence is in the first lesson.

Subject and level depth means the tutor can move between the syllabus and the paper. In Mathematics, that includes proof, calculus and unfamiliar problems for Analysis and Approaches (AA), or modelling and statistics with a graphic display calculator for Applications and Interpretation (AI). In the sciences, it means handling data-based questions and the investigation that forms the Internal Assessment (IA). In Economics, it means commentary writing and diagrams used as arguments.

Assessment familiarity means that the tutor reads questions as a marker does. They know that command terms set the depth of an answer, that method marks are earned on the page, and that the IA, the Extended Essay (EE) and Theory of Knowledge (TOK) are each governed by integrity rules. A tutor who talks only about "covering chapters" is describing a classroom, not an assessment.

An honest diagnostic is the third filter. A tutor who proposes a plan before looking at a recent script or test is guessing. A good first conversation asks to see marked work, asks which paper or criterion loses the most marks, and says what the tutor would not be able to help with.

The fourth is practical: a schedule that survives Faridabad's weekday evenings, school transport, the school's calendar and exam seasons. The rest of this post expands each point, and the [how to choose an IB maths tutor](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-ib-maths-tutor-in-gurgaon/) guide has an interview script that applies to any subject, not only maths.

## What Does a First Trial Lesson Look Like?

A good trial lesson is a working lesson, not a sales demonstration. It lasts as long as a normal session and uses the student's own material: a recent test, a topic the student finds hard, or a past-paper question. IB Gram can arrange a demo or trial session before a family commits, subject to tutor availability, and the family can ask for a different match if the fit is wrong.

The sequence below is a useful checklist for parents who sit in or review afterwards.

1. **The tutor asks questions first.** About the school, subject, level, the recent mark and what the student finds hardest.
2. **The tutor looks at real work.** A marked paper, a unit test or an IA outline. A tutor who never looks at work is not diagnosing.
3. **The student does something.** Solves a problem, explains an idea, drafts a paragraph. A lesson in which only the tutor speaks is a lecture.
4. **The tutor names a cause.** "You lose marks because the working skips the setup" is concrete. "You need more practice" is not.
5. **There is a plan for the next two weeks.** Short, specific and linked to the assessment.
6. **The student can say what they learned.** Ask them in the car or at dinner.

If the trial does not produce at least a sharper picture of the problem, it is a reasonable signal to try another tutor. The [IB tutor interview and review guide](/blog/how-to-choose-ib-tutor-gurgaon/) lists questions to ask in the first call and what to look for in the first month.

## How Can You Judge a Tutor Properly, Not Just Hire the Best-Sounding One?

You judge a tutor on evidence you can see, and the word "best" in a search result is not evidence. IB Gram makes no claim to be the best, and neither should you accept one from anyone. What you can check is below.

:::steps The evidence ladder for judging a tutor
Subject | Can they solve and explain your syllabus?
Marks | Do they know where IB marks are earned?
Diagnosis | Do they read your child's work first?
Boundaries | Do they respect IA integrity rules?
Review | Do they report progress and adapt?
:::

**Subject.** Ask the tutor to work a past-paper question aloud, not to recite credentials. Ask what they would do if the student's answer were half right.

**Marks.** Ask how method marks, command terms or IA criteria work in the subject. A confident, specific answer is a good sign; vagueness is not.

**Diagnosis.** A reliable tutor asks for marked work before proposing a plan. If they quote a fixed plan without looking, treat it as a template.

**Boundaries.** The IB's academic integrity policy allows a teacher to give feedback on one IA draft; it does not allow anyone, tutor or parent, to write, rewrite or edit the student's submission. A tutor may teach the skill, question the reasoning, point at a criterion and ask the student to reread their own paragraph. A tutor who offers to "do" the IA or promises an IA outcome is a risk to the student. The full rules are in the [IB Internal Assessment guide](/blog/ib-internal-assessment-guide/).

**Review.** Ask how progress will be shown after a month. Good answers include marked samples, a short written note per week or a clear change in error patterns. Avoid vague claims about "improvement".

No tutor can guarantee a grade, and any provider who does is a warning sign. What you can reasonably expect is better diagnosis, clearer feedback and a routine that holds.

## What Can a Tutor Do for DP Mathematics Students in Faridabad?

Mathematics is the most requested DP subject, and the tutoring is usually about one of a few specific problems. A student may have hidden algebra weakness that slows every unit; a student may know the content and lose marks on written working; a student may be in AA HL and stall at Paper 3; or a student in AI may need confidence with the graphic display calculator and modelling.

The structure of the assessment guides the work. In Mathematics AA HL, Paper 1 (no calculator) and Paper 2 (calculator) are each 30%, Paper 3 is 20% and the IA is 20%. The IA exploration is the same task across AA and AI, SL and HL: 12 to 20 pages, marked out of 20 on five criteria (Presentation, Mathematical communication, Personal engagement, Reflection, Use of mathematics), with HL work judged against a higher standard on the last. A tutor can teach the mathematics an exploration needs; they cannot write it.

## What About Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Economics?

The sciences share a structure. DP sciences (first assessed 2025) have Paper 1 in two parts, 1A multiple choice and 1B data-based questions, and Paper 2 with short-answer and extended-response questions. The IA is a scientific investigation worth 20%, marked on four criteria of 6 marks each: Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion and Evaluation, up to 3,000 words. Students may investigate in groups of up to three, but each writes an individual report. HL and SL share the assessment structure; HL adds Additional Higher Level (AHL) content.

In practice, tutoring in sciences helps with four things. Calculation fluency, such as stoichiometry or kinematics. Data-based question technique. Understanding uncertainty and graphing for the investigation. And clear written explanations for extended-response questions. The service pages for [Physics](/ib-tutors/faridabad/physics/) and [Chemistry](/ib-tutors/faridabad/chemistry/) in Faridabad show how these are matched, and the Gurgaon-focused guides on [IB Physics HL study plans](/blog/ib-physics-hl-most-important-topics-study-plan/) and [IB Chemistry HL vs SL](/blog/ib-chemistry-tutor-in-gurgaon-hl-vs-sl-support-guide/) are useful reading for the subject content, as the syllabus is the same wherever you live.

Biology is organised into four themes (A Unity and diversity, B Form and function, C Interaction and interdependence, D Continuity and change) studied across four levels of organisation: molecules, cells, organisms and ecosystems. Students often find the breadth heavy, and a tutor helps by building links across themes and practising data questions.

Economics has four units (Introduction to economics, Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, The global economy). Paper 1 is extended response, Paper 2 is data response, and Paper 3, a policy paper, is HL only. The IA is a portfolio of three commentaries, each up to 800 words, on published news articles, each drawing on a different syllabus unit and a different one of the nine key concepts. A tutor can help a student learn to build diagrams into arguments and to read news articles analytically; the commentaries must be the student's own. ## When Is a Tutor Not the Right Answer?

Sometimes a tutor is not the right answer, and a trustworthy source says so. If a student is overloaded, with six subjects, the EE, TOK, CAS and a heavy extracurricular load, a tutor adds another appointment to an already full week. The right first step may be a conversation with the DP coordinator about workload and a rebuilt timetable.

If the problem is attendance or motivation, a tutor can help only if the student wants to engage. If the student is already scoring close to their target and the main issue is anxiety, a counsellor or a conversation with the school may be more useful than extra lessons. And if the problem is the school's teaching in a particular subject, the first move is a polite conversation with the teacher, since teachers often have help available such as drop-in hours or practice sets.

A tutor makes the most difference when there is a specific, diagnosable gap and the student is willing to practise between sessions. Where the diagnostic reveals that the real issue is something else, a good tutor will say so.

## How Do Exam Seasons Change the Plan in Faridabad?

IB schools in India almost all enter candidates for the May session; November is used by a small minority and by retake candidates. Each school sets its own DP2 mock examination dates, usually several months before May, so your child's mock calendar is the one that matters. Results for May are released in early July.

The year has predictable pressure points. In the first term of DP1, the work is settling in: subject adjustment, notation, pace. In the second half of DP1, IA topic selection begins and the EE starts. In DP2, the first term is full of IA and EE deadlines, followed by mocks and then a long run-in to May. A tutor schedule that ignores this will fail when it matters.

A sensible pattern is a steady weekly session through DP1, a lighter weekly rhythm during mock weeks to protect rest, and a more intensive paper-focused block between the mocks and the exams. The [IB exam preparation hub](/blog/ib-exam-preparation-guide/) lays out how the exam year fits together, and the [IB DP core timeline](/blog/ib-dp-core-planning-timeline/) shows how CAS, the EE and TOK interact with subject work. ## What About Students Switching from Another Board?

Students moving into the IB from CBSE, ICSE or a state board are common in Faridabad. The usual differences a switching student meets are criterion-referenced or command-term marking instead of a fixed answer key, coursework and internal assessment that carry real weight, wider reading and independent research, and less emphasis on memorised derivations. For a student in this position, the first term is a transition and the early test scores should be read as feedback.

A tutor with experience of both systems can save weeks. They can show how an answer that earns full marks in a board paper may lose marks in a command-term question that asks for evaluation. They can build the habit of reading the verb first. They can introduce the IA early, so that it does not arrive as a shock. Families should treat the first six to eight weeks as a settling period and judge progress by the quality of the questions the student asks and the clarity of their written working, not only by marks.

If a student is coming from IGCSE rather than an Indian board, the maths gap in particular is well covered in [the IGCSE to IB DP maths transition](/blog/from-igcse-mathematics-to-ib-dp-building-a-strong-foundation/).

## What Does a Good First Month With a Tutor Look Like?

A good first month has a shape. Week 1 is diagnosis: the tutor reviews recent papers, runs a short skills check and names the one or two causes that matter most. Week 2 starts the repair: a routine of short practice, with written working and a record of errors. Week 3 adds timed practice or the first IA planning conversation, depending on the stage. Week 4 is a review: what changed in the error pattern, and what the next month focuses on.

:::timeline The first month with a new IB tutor
Week 1 | Review marked work, name the main causes
Week 2 | Start repair routine, short practice, written working
Week 3 | Add timed practice or IA planning
Week 4 | Review progress, adjust or switch
:::

Parents can support without hovering. Ask the student what they learned, not what they covered. Check that the weekly slot is kept. Ask the tutor for a short note after each week or two, and watch whether the student starts attempting harder questions. If after a month the picture is no clearer and nothing has changed in the error pattern, ask for a different match. ## What About Siblings and Several Subjects in One Household?

Households with two IB students, or one student needing support in two or three subjects, have a scheduling puzzle. The practical answer is to prioritise by marks at risk and by timing. A student with a Mathematics HL problem and a Biology SL problem should usually start with Mathematics, because the content builds on itself, and add Biology closer to the exam if the problem remains.

Where several subjects need help, ask whether one tutor can cover related subjects, for example Physics and Mathematics, which often share problem-solving skills. Avoid splitting the week into too many sessions with different people, because the overhead of context-switching and the student's fatigue can outweigh the benefit. For siblings at different stages, a shared evening schedule can work if the tutor arrives once for two sessions back to back, though each student deserves their own focused time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are there good IB tutors in Faridabad?

Yes, though the pool is smaller than in Gurgaon, so matching matters more. Good tutors show subject depth, understand how the IB marks and read your child's work before proposing a plan. For rarer subjects or Higher Level options you may need an online tutor from outside Faridabad. A trial lesson is the best test of any tutor.

### Can IB subjects be taught properly online from Faridabad?

Yes, most of them. Mathematics works well with a shared whiteboard or a tablet, the sciences work for theory, calculations and data questions, and Economics and English work well with shared documents. Hands-on practical work stays with the school. Check the tutor's setup in the trial: audio quality, writing tools and a way to share the student's written work.

### Is home tuition or online tuition better in Faridabad?

Neither is better in general. Home tuition suits younger students and those who focus better away from a screen, while online suits busy schedules, rare subjects and exam-season flexibility. Many families use a hybrid, with weekday sessions online and a weekend session at home. Try a format for a month, review and change it if it is not working.

### How do I judge whether an IB tutor is any good?

Look for evidence, not claims. Ask them to solve and explain a past-paper question, describe how marks or criteria work in your subject and review a recent marked script before proposing a plan. Good tutors name specific causes of lost marks and respect IA integrity rules. Ask how progress will be shown after a month, and treat any grade guarantee as a warning.

### Can a tutor help with my IB Internal Assessment?

A tutor may teach the underlying skill, question your reasoning and point you to a criterion so you can reread your own paragraph. A tutor may not write, rewrite or edit your submission, and the same goes for parents. The school submits the work and runs similarity checking. If anyone offers to complete an IA for you, walk away.

### Which IB subjects do Faridabad students ask for most?

Mathematics, in both AA and AI at both levels, is the most requested, followed by Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Economics. Support for the Internal Assessment and Extended Essay skills is common, and some families look for English support, including English B. Availability varies by subject and tutor, and rarer combinations may need an online tutor.

### My child is switching to IB from CBSE. When should we start tutoring?

Start in the first term if the student is struggling with command-term marking, written working or the pace, and not later than the first signs of a falling test score. The first six to eight weeks are a settling period, so judge progress by the quality of written work and questions. A tutor who knows both systems can help bridge the differences.

### How many sessions a week do we need?

Most students need one or two sessions a week for a specific subject, with more only during a defined push such as the run-up to mocks. More sessions do not help if the student does not practise between them. Ask the tutor what the student should do between sessions, and expect that to be as important as the lesson.

### What if the tutor is not working out?

Say so early, preferably after the trial or within the first month, and ask for a different match. IB Gram lets families ask for a different match, and a demo session can be arranged before committing. A good tutor will welcome a review conversation. Before switching, check whether the issue is the tutor, the schedule or something outside tutoring such as workload.

## What Should Working Parents Near Cyber City Look For in an IB Tutor?

Working parents near Cyber City should look for a tutor who can deliver three things beyond subject knowledge: reliable late-afternoon or evening availability, a written update habit after each session, and a way of working that does not need a parent present. A tutor who is brilliant but cannot keep a schedule, or who explains everything verbally and leaves no record, is a poor fit for a household that cannot supervise.

Subject expertise still comes first. An IB tutor should know the programme the student is in, whether MYP criteria or DP papers and components, and the level (Higher Level, HL, or Standard Level, SL). The tutor should be able to say what the student's school is likely to assess in the next four weeks. But a household where nobody sits in adds a second test: can the tutor run a session in which the student does most of the work, and can they tell you afterwards, in four lines, what happened?

That second test is the one most families skip. It is the one that decides whether tutoring in a working-parent home improves anything or becomes a regular, expensive hour of a student watching someone else solve problems.

**Evening traffic limits a tutor's travel window.** A tutor coming to your home on a weekday evening is crossing Gurgaon at the same time as everyone else. A 6:00 pm start can drift to 6:20 pm or later. Plan for a start time with a buffer, and agree what happens if a tutor is more than fifteen minutes late.

**Late-evening slots are common, and sensible.** Many working-parent households settle on a start between 6:30 pm and 8:00 pm, when the student is back from school, has eaten and has finished or nearly finished homework. **Gate entry is part of logistics.** Most societies need the visitor registered with security, often with a phone call to the resident. If no adult is home, make an arrangement in advance: a standing instruction at the gate, or a household helper with the authority to approve the tutor. Without it, sessions begin late or fail at the gate.

**Study space is a real variable.** Apartments in this part of Gurgaon vary widely. If the student's desk is in a bedroom, the family has to decide whether a tutor works there or at a dining table, and whether a door is open. The matter is not a luxury. It decides whether a session feels like work or like a visit.

## The Update Routine: What to Ask a Tutor to Send

The single most useful thing a working parent can ask a tutor for is a short written update after every session. Without it, a household that cannot sit in has no evidence of what is happening, and the family's only signal is the next report card, which arrives too late to change anything.

Ask for four lines, sent the same evening:

1. **Covered.** What was worked on, in a sentence.
2. **Found.** The one pattern or error that appeared.
3. **Set.** The task for the next session or the week between, with a size (for example, "six questions, about 30 minutes").
4. **Watch.** One thing the parent should know, such as an upcoming deadline or a worry.

A tutor who objects to four lines of text is a poor sign. A tutor who sends a page of vague praise is also a poor sign. The test is whether the update says something specific that a different tutor could act on.

Once a month, ask for a longer note: where the student stands against the plan, what has changed, and what the next four weeks look like. That one is worth ten minutes of reading, and it is the right occasion to compare the tutor's view with the school's.

### A parent's own routine

On your side, keep the response small but regular. Reading the update, replying with a thumbs-up or one question, and glancing at the student's task list takes five minutes. The point is that the tutor and student know someone reads it.

## Safeguards That Sensible Families Set

When a tutor is in the home with nobody else present, or with a teenager alone at a screen, a family should set simple rules in advance. These are ordinary precautions, not signs of distrust.

- **Verify who is coming.** Ask for the tutor's name, a photograph and identification, and share it with the gate and a trusted adult. IB Gram shortlists tutors whose profiles are verified, but the family should still follow its own routine for who enters its home.
- **Have an adult reachable.** A grandparent, a helper, a neighbour or a parent on a phone, with the student knowing who it is.
- **Keep the door arrangement clear.** Sessions in a shared room, with the door open, are a reasonable default.
- **Agree on communication channels.** Tutor and student communicate about schedule and tasks through a channel a parent can see, such as a family group, not private direct messages.
- **For online sessions, check the setup.** Camera on, a shared screen the parent can occasionally look at, no unrecorded side channels, and an agreed end time.

None of this needs to be heavy. A short conversation with the tutor in the demo session sets the tone, and a good tutor will welcome it.

## What to Ask a Tutor When You Cannot Be in the Room

A working parent gets one trial session, and sometimes cannot attend it. The questions below are for a call or message before or after.

1. **How will you run a session so the student does most of the work?**
2. **What will you send me after each session, and when?**
3. **What do you need from the school (marked work, criteria, calendar) before the first lesson?**
4. **How do you handle a student who goes quiet or says "I understand" when they do not?**
5. **What will you do about the Internal Assessment, and what will you not do?**
6. **What is your cancellation and make-up routine?** Evening traffic and work travel mean sessions will occasionally be moved.
7. **When would you tell us to stop or reduce sessions?**

The sixth question matters more than it seems. Families with unpredictable work hours need a clear, fair routine for rescheduling, set in writing, so that the tutoring does not collapse after two missed weeks.

## IA, Extended Essay and Academic Integrity When You Are Not Watching

Working parents sometimes worry that a student left alone with a tutor might be helped too much with an Internal Assessment (IA) or the Extended Essay (EE). The worry is reasonable, and the rules are clear. Every IB internal assessment is the student's own work. The IB's academic integrity policy allows a teacher to give feedback on one draft; it does not allow anyone, tutor or parent, to write, rewrite or edit the student's submission. A tutor may teach the underlying skill, question the reasoning, point at a criterion and ask the student to reread their own paragraph against it. The school submits the work and runs it through similarity checking, and malpractice findings are handled by the IB, not the school alone.

For a household that cannot supervise, this means two practical steps. State the rule in the first conversation with the tutor, in front of the student. And ask the tutor to describe, in the update, what skill was practised on IA days, not what text was produced. Any tutor who offers to "just fix" a draft is a risk to the student's diploma. The wider process is covered in the [IB Internal Assessment guide](/blog/ib-internal-assessment-guide/), and an overview of the Extended Essay is in [how to write an IB Extended Essay](/blog/how-to-write-a-perfect-ib-extended-essay-ee/).

## What Goes Wrong, and What Should You Do About It?

:::steps A fix-it sequence for a drifting plan
Notice | Updates thin out or sessions slip
Ask | One direct message about the cause
Reset | Agree the update format and slot again
Decide | Keep, change tutor or reduce hours
:::

1. **The updates stop.** Message the tutor once, directly, naming what you need. If the updates do not resume within a week, ask for a different match.
2. **The sessions keep moving.** Agree on a fixed slot and a written reschedule rule. If either side cannot keep it, the slot is wrong.
3. **The student says it is going fine, and marks are flat.** Ask the tutor for the error log and a short review. Compare it with the school's returned work.
4. **The student works well with the tutor but not alone.** The plan may need smaller between-session tasks, or a short fixed study block with a parent checking in by message.
5. **The student is tired.** The plan is too heavy. Reduce, do not push.

The common thread is that the family does not need to be present to notice any of these, provided the update routine is in place.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get an IB tutor near Cyber City Gurgaon for a late-evening slot?

Often yes, subject to tutor availability. Many tutors offer slots between 6:30 pm and 8:00 pm on weekdays, and online sessions make later or more flexible times easier because the tutor does not have to cross the city. Say your preferred window in the first query, since evening slots fill first, and agree a buffer for traffic if the session is at home.

### Is online or home tutoring better if both parents work?

Online is usually the easier weekday option because it removes tutor travel and does not need an adult to host. Home sessions suit paper-based work and families who want the tutor to see the student's writing. Many working-parent households choose weekday online sessions with an occasional weekend home session, and adjust by subject and season.

### What if nobody is home when the tutor arrives?

Do not leave it to chance. Arrange a standing instruction at the gate, or name a household helper or relative who can receive the tutor, and share the tutor's details with them. For a student alone at home with a home tutor, set the safeguards above: verified identity, an adult reachable, and a clear communication channel a parent can see.

### How do I know the sessions are working if I cannot attend?

Ask for a four-line written update after each session and a longer monthly note, and compare them with the school's returned work. A tutor who can name the pattern found and the task set is working from evidence. Judge progress by school assessments, criterion marks and timed papers, not by the student's mood after a session.

### Should my MYP child have tutoring alone at home?

For younger MYP students, it helps to have an adult in the house and a tutor who also teaches organisation, not just content. MYP 4 and 5 students are usually more independent. The decision depends on the child. Online sessions with a parent working in another room are a common compromise, and the tutor's updates keep you informed.

### My DP child has an IA due and I cannot supervise. What should the tutor do?

The tutor should teach the skills behind the IA and question the student's reasoning, but must not write, rewrite or edit the work. The IB allows a teacher feedback on one draft, and malpractice findings are handled by the IB. State the rule in the first conversation, and ask the tutor to report in the update which skill was practised on IA days.

### What should the parent update routine look like?

Keep it short and regular. Ask the tutor for four lines after each session covering what was done, what was found, what is set for next time and anything to watch. Reply briefly so tutor and student know it is read. Once a month, ask for a longer review against the plan. Five minutes of your attention a week is enough.

### How should we handle missed or moved sessions?

Agree a written routine at the start: how much notice is expected, how make-ups are arranged and what happens after two moved sessions in a row. Working-parent schedules and Gurgaon traffic make occasional moves normal. If sessions move repeatedly, the slot is wrong, and the answer is a different time or mode, not guilt.

### Do you have tutors for every IB subject near Cyber City?

Not necessarily every subject at every time. IB Gram matches by subject, programme, level, schedule and mode, and availability is subject to tutor availability. Online sessions widen the pool because the tutor need not be nearby. Share the exact subject and level, such as Chemistry HL or MYP 4 Mathematics, in the query so the shortlist is relevant.

### Is a tutor worth it if my child is already doing fine?

Often not, or only briefly. If assessments show a steady trajectory and the student can name their own error patterns, extra tuition may add load without adding value. A short block before mock exams, or a single-topic fix, can be enough. An honest tutor will say so, and a working-parent household has little time to waste.

### Can my younger child and my DP child share a tutor?

Sometimes, if the tutor teaches both programmes and subjects, but usually the two students need different tutors because the subjects and levels differ. Sessions placed back to back reduce the number of visits. Ask in the query whether the family has more than one student, and the shortlist can consider schedule fit.

## What Does Vasant Kunj Look Like From a DP Family's Point of View?

Vasant Kunj is a large residential colony of lettered sectors (A, B, C and D) with clusters of flats and independent houses, a mix of older apartment blocks and newer buildings, and a regular flow of traffic along its main roads. For a DP family, the practical features are these:

- **Many homes are in gated blocks or societies with a guard and a visitor log.** A tutor arriving at six in the evening may need a few minutes at the gate, and some societies want the family to pre-register the visitor.
- **Study space varies a great deal.** A student in a large house may have a quiet room; a student in a compact flat may share a hall with a younger sibling, which changes whether a two-hour lesson is workable.
- **The area is close to the airport corridor and to the road towards Gurgaon.** That convenience helps families who travel, but it also means weekday evening traffic can lengthen any commute, for tutor and student alike.

The sectors themselves make less difference than you might expect. What matters is the visit: which gate, which floor, which road the tutor must use at that hour. A tutor based nearby, or one whose other lessons are close, will usually be more reliable than a tutor with a better profile who crosses the city twice a week. We do not state distances or travel times here because they depend on the hour and the route, and a family knows them better than any guide.

## Why Do Vasant Kunj Families Often Straddle Two Cities?

Because the area is a natural meeting point between south Delhi and the Gurgaon corridor. A child may attend an IB school in Delhi while a parent commutes to Gurgaon; another child may attend a school south of the border and live in Vasant Kunj. Cousins, friends and classmates are spread across both.

This matters for tutoring in three ways.

**The school calendar may follow Gurgaon or Delhi patterns.** IB schools in India overwhelmingly enter DP candidates for the May session and run an academic year of roughly April to March, but each school sets its own mock dates, holiday weeks and IA deadlines. A tutor must work from your child's school calendar, not a general one.

**Friends and study groups are in another city.** An evening lesson that finishes at an awkward hour can cut across the only window the student has to meet a lab partner or a classmate for a joint IA.

**A move is always possible.** Families who relocate within the NCR do not want to change tutors. A tutor who can teach online as easily as at home lets the programme continue through a move, a holiday or a long traffic week.

## Which DP Subjects Benefit Most From a Home Tutor Here?

A home tutor adds most where a student's problem is specific and observable on paper: Mathematics, the sciences, Economics and coursework such as the IA and EE. Subjects where the issue is fluency in a language or wide reading need a different kind of support.

:::stats DP structure to keep in mind
6 | Subjects from six groups
3-4 | Taken at Higher Level
45 | Maximum diploma points
24 | Minimum points for the diploma
:::

The DP is a two-year course (DP1 and DP2) of six subjects, three or four at HL (240 recommended teaching hours) and the rest at SL (150 hours). Each subject is graded 1 to 7. Theory of Knowledge and the Extended Essay combine for up to 3 more points, so the maximum is 45, and 24 points is the minimum for the diploma, subject to the IB's failing conditions. Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS) must also be completed.

### Mathematics

Mathematics comes in two subjects, each at HL and SL: Analysis and Approaches (AA), which is algebraic and proof-heavy, and Applications and Interpretation (AI), which is modelling and statistics heavy with technology used throughout. Students in this area who are heading for engineering, economics or physical sciences are often advised towards AA, but the choice depends on the degree and the student, and the school's DP coordinator makes the final placement. For AA HL, see [IB Math AA HL tutor in Gurgaon](/blog/ib-math-aa-hl-tutor-gurgaon-guide/) for the tutor checklist, and the [IB Maths AA HL tutors in Delhi](/ib-tutors/delhi/math-aa-hl/) page. For AI HL, see [IB Math AI HL tutor in Gurgaon](/blog/ib-math-ai-hl-tutor-gurgaon-guide/). Any IB maths student will find [how to score a 7 in IB Math AA HL](/blog/how-to-score-7-ib-math-aa-hl/) a useful map of what marks are awarded for.

### Sciences

The DP sciences, first assessed in 2025, have a Paper 1 in two parts (1A multiple choice and 1B data-based questions) and a Paper 2 with short-answer and extended-response questions. The IA is a scientific investigation worth 20%, marked on four criteria of 6 marks each (Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion and Evaluation), with a maximum of 3,000 words. HL and SL share the assessment structure, and HL adds Additional Higher Level (AHL) content. A tutor can help with calculations, with unfamiliar data questions and with the discipline of the write-up. For subject pages, see [IB Physics, Chemistry and Biology](/ib-tutors/delhi/physics/) support in Delhi and the Gurgaon-based guides on [IB Physics HL](/blog/ib-physics-hl-tutor-gurgaon/), [IB Chemistry HL](/blog/ib-chemistry-hl-tutor-gurgaon/) and [IB Biology HL](/blog/ib-biology-hl-tutor-gurgaon/).

### Economics

DP Economics has four units (Introduction to economics, Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, The global economy). Paper 1 is extended response, Paper 2 is data response, and Paper 3, a policy paper, is HL only. The internal assessment is a portfolio of three commentaries on published news articles, each up to 800 words. A tutor adds value by training students to apply a diagram and a concept to a specific article, which is a different skill from knowing the theory. See [IB Economics tutors in Delhi](/ib-tutors/delhi/economics/).

### English and other subjects

Students whose first language is not English take English B, and the others take English A Language and Literature or English A Literature. These respond to practice with feedback, and a tutor can help with textual analysis and structured essay writing. Languages and Individuals and Societies subjects have their own formats; the school's subject guide is the authority.

## What Can a Tutor Do for the IA, the EE and TOK?

A tutor can teach the skills behind coursework, but cannot write or edit the student's submission. Every IB internal assessment is the student's own work, and the IB's academic integrity policy allows a teacher to give feedback on one draft. It does not allow anyone, tutor or parent, to write, rewrite or edit the student's submission.

In practice, a tutor may teach the underlying skill, question the reasoning, point at a criterion and ask the student to reread their own paragraph against it. The school submits the work and runs it through similarity checking, and malpractice findings are handled by the IB, not the school alone. A tutor who offers to "do the IA" is putting your child's diploma at risk.

For the Extended Essay, the supervisor is a teacher at the student's own school, and the essay is up to 4,000 words and roughly 40 hours of work. Tutors can help with research methods, reading, planning and structure, never with writing the text. The EE guide was updated for first assessment in 2027, so follow the version your DP coordinator issues. For TOK, the exhibition (three objects linked to one of 35 IA prompts, up to 950 words) and the essay (one of six prescribed titles, up to 1,600 words) both reward clear thinking about knowledge claims; a tutor can help a student practise that in conversation.

Both work, and many families use both. Home lessons suit students who write best on paper with a tutor beside them and who have a quiet space. Online lessons suit evenings when traffic is heavy, weeks when the student is travelling, and subjects where screen sharing helps, such as data work in AI maths or graph work in Economics.

## How Do You Choose IB Tutors in Vasant Kunj?

Choose IB tutors in Vasant Kunj the way you would choose anywhere: by subject depth, diagnostic method, understanding of the school calendar and clarity about academic integrity, then check logistics. Do not choose by the sound of the profile or the number of subjects listed.

1. **Name the real problem.** "Maths" is not a problem; "loses marks on Paper 1 algebra" or "cannot start the IA" is.
2. **Ask for a diagnostic first.** A good tutor wants to see a recent test or paper before proposing a plan.
3. **Check subject and level.** HL is not SL, and AA is not AI. A tutor should know the difference without being asked.
4. **Ask about the school's calendar.** The tutor should plan around your child's mock dates and IA deadlines, not a generic timeline.
5. **Clarify integrity.** The tutor should tell you, unprompted, what they will not do with coursework.
6. **Agree the format and the first month.** Home, online or hybrid, with a review after four weeks.
7. **Trial before committing.** A demo session shows teaching style and fit.

The selection process in detail, including interview questions, is in [how to choose the right IB maths tutor in Gurgaon](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-ib-maths-tutor-in-gurgaon/) and, for the wider view, [how to choose an IB tutor in Gurgaon](/blog/how-to-choose-ib-tutor-gurgaon/). Both apply directly to Delhi families.

### Qualities that matter more than a long profile

- Can explain a concept three different ways without being asked.
- Watches the student work before correcting.
- Keeps a record of the student's errors and shows it to the family.
- Says no when a request is wrong, such as writing coursework.
- Is honest about what they do not teach.

## A Typical Weekly Pattern for a Vasant Kunj DP Student

A pattern that works for many students is two lessons a week in one or two subjects, one of them scheduled when traffic is lightest, with a short independent practice set between lessons. The shape below is an example, not a prescription.

Weekday evening | Online lesson, 60 minutes, one subject

| Phase | Priority | Tutoring emphasis |

## What Do Families Between Delhi and Gurgaon Get Wrong?

They tend to make the same few decisions, and each is easy to fix.

**Choosing a tutor by neighbourhood name rather than fit.** A tutor in the same sector who does not know HL Chemistry is less useful than an online tutor who does. Location is a logistics question, not a quality one.

**Booking the same hour as school activities.** An evening lesson that regularly competes with sport or a club will be cancelled, and a plan that is cancelled half the time is not a plan.

**Starting in the panic month.** Families often begin tutoring just before mocks. Starting earlier and lighter produces better habits. See [when to start IB tutoring in Gurgaon](/blog/when-to-start-ib-tutoring-gurgaon/); the timing logic is the same in Delhi.

**Overloading the week.** Five subjects with a tutor each leaves no time to think. Choose the one or two subjects where marks are most recoverable.

**Treating the tutor as a deadline rescue for coursework.** A tutor who is introduced three weeks before an IA deadline can teach very little that the student can use.

**Forgetting the weekend commute.** Weekend traffic near malls and highways can be heavy, and a tutor travelling at the wrong hour arrives late and tired.

### Is a home tutor or an online tutor better in Vasant Kunj?

Neither is better for everyone. Home suits students who work best on paper and have a quiet room, while online suits busy school nights and avoids traffic.

Many families use online on weekdays and home lessons at weekends. A trial session in each format shows which works for your child.

### Can a tutor help with my child's IA?

A tutor can teach the skills, question your child's reasoning and point to the criteria. A tutor cannot write, rewrite or edit the IA.

The IB's academic integrity policy allows a teacher to give feedback on one draft, and the work must be the student's own. Help is most useful early, when choosing a question and planning the method.

### Do I need a tutor in every DP subject?

No. Most students benefit from support in one or two subjects where marks are recoverable, and managing more usually overloads the week.

Pick subjects by where marks are being lost and why. If the cause is not clear, a diagnostic from a tutor can help you decide.

### My child's school is in Gurgaon but we live in Vasant Kunj. Does that matter?

It matters for the schedule, not the quality. The tutor should plan around the school's calendar and your child's actual evening availability.

Online lessons can avoid the commute for the tutor and for you, and the plan can continue if you move within the NCR.

### How do I know the tutor knows HL, not just SL?

Ask the tutor to describe what HL adds in the subject and to work through an HL-style question during the trial session.

HL carries 240 recommended teaching hours against 150 at SL, and the extension content is not a small difference. A tutor who cannot explain it clearly is probably not a good HL match.

### When should we start DP tutoring?

Early in DP1 is usually better than waiting for a crisis, because habits and fluency take months to build. Starting lightly is better than starting in panic.

If your child is already in DP2, start now with a diagnostic. See [when to start IB tutoring](/blog/when-to-start-ib-tutoring-gurgaon/).

### What if the tutor is not a good fit after a few lessons?

Say so, and ask for a different match. IB Gram lets families request one, subject to tutor availability.

Review at the end of the first month: is there a written diagnosis, an error log and a plan? If not, change tutor rather than wait.

### Will a tutor get my child a 7 or the diploma?

No. No tutor can guarantee a grade. A good tutor changes how quickly the causes of lost marks are found and fixed.

Be wary of anyone who promises results. Judge by diagnosis, plan, communication and the student's growing independence.

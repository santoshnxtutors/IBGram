# Gurgaon page standard (read with WRITER-GUIDE.md)

Every page must meet all of this. An automated gate rejects failures.

## Uniqueness
- This site has hundreds of Gurgaon IB/IGCSE tutoring pages covering the SAME locality for other subjects and the SAME subject for other localities. An automated check compares every page against all of them and rejects anything too similar. Your 3 pages must not share sentences with each other either.
- Build each page from what is specific to THAT subject at THAT level in THAT locality: the brief's corridor, nearby areas, sectors, societies and schools; real syllabus papers, components, criteria, syllabus codes and exam sessions; the brief's angle and section plan.

## Length
- More than 4,000 words of body prose per page (hard floor). Aim for 4,300-4,800.
- Exactly 9 sections, each with exactly 4 paragraphs of 110-160 words plus 4-5 bullets.
- 10-12 FAQs, each answer 80-130 words.
- Length comes from NEW information, never restatement.

## SEO (rank for every way a Gurgaon parent searches)
- The FIRST sentence of heroIntro names both the subject (or board) and the locality. heroIntro is 90-130 words.
- The locality name appears in at least 3 of the 9 H2 headings.
- Work at least 8 of the brief's 12 secondaryKeywords into headings or body naturally, plus the natural search variants: "home tutor", "home tuition", "tutor", "tuition", "classes", "Gurgaon", "Gurugram", the board alone (IB / IGCSE) and the board with the subject.

## AEO / GEO (written to be quoted by AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini)
- At least 3 of the 9 H2 headings are phrased as the question a parent asks, and the first 40-60 words under each answer it directly and completely.
- One numbered step-by-step process (how to start, or how matching works) that makes sense if quoted alone.
- One direct comparison of home, online and hybrid tutoring for this subject in this locality, readable on its own.
- Spell entities in full once, then abbreviate: International Baccalaureate (IB), Diploma Programme (DP), Middle Years Programme (MYP), Primary Years Programme (PYP), Cambridge Assessment International Education, Pearson Edexcel, Higher Level (HL), Standard Level (SL), Internal Assessment (IA), Extended Essay (EE), Theory of Knowledge (TOK).
- State checkable facts in extractable form: exam session months, paper and component names, syllabus codes, grade scales (IB 1-7, IGCSE 9-1 or A*-G), tiers or levels.
- Every FAQ answer opens with a one-sentence direct answer, then the detail.
- Write "Gurugram (Gurgaon)" at least once and name Haryana once.

## Honesty and voice
- Never use "That said,", "crucially,", "when it comes to", "in conclusion", "in essence", or anything else on the guide's banned list.
- One sentence states IB Gram is an independent platform, not affiliated with or endorsed by the schools or boards named. Never claim a partnership with any school, the IB, Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel.
- No tutor counts, no guaranteed grades, no invented statistics or reviews, no rupee fee amounts.

## Output
Keys exactly: slug (unchanged, from the brief), heroIntro, trustPoints (4), sections (9 of {heading, paragraphs, bullets}), faqs, closingCta, localKeywords (10-12).

## Hard rules
- One Write call per page, and each output must be valid JSON.
- Never read a page back after writing it, never grep it or count its words, run no builds, tests or lint, never touch src/.
